The only free trial Midjourney's own help centre lists today is "a limited trial" in the niji journey app for iOS and Android, per its [Free Trials article](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27870399340173-Free-Trials), last updated on **10 December 2025**. For everyone else, is Midjourney free on midjourney.com or in Discord? No: the same article says "No free trial is currently available in Discord or the midjourney.com website," and the cheapest plan on the website is **$10 a month**.

My view after reading Midjourney's help centre on **31 October 2026**: for a marketer making campaign images, the useful number is $24, the monthly cost of Standard on yearly billing, because Standard is the cheapest plan where image generation stops being metered.

## Is Midjourney Free?

No. Midjourney has no free plan, and its docs say there is no free trial on the website or in Discord. The one exception is a limited trial in the niji journey mobile app. Everything else on the web or Discord needs a subscription, from Basic at $10 a month to Mega at $120, with 20% off yearly.

## Midjourney Plans and Prices in USD

Midjourney's [Comparing Midjourney Plans](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27870484040333-Comparing-Midjourney-Plans) article, last updated 18 May 2026, lists four subscriptions:

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (paid upfront) | Fast GPU time | Relax GPU time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $10 | $96 ($8 a month) | 3.3 hr a month (200 minutes) | None |
| Standard | $30 | $288 ($24 a month) | 15 hr a month | Unlimited images |
| Pro | $60 | $576 ($48 a month) | 30 hr a month | Unlimited images and SD video |
| Mega | $120 | $1,152 ($96 a month) | 60 hr a month | Unlimited images and SD video |

Other lines from the same article that change which plan fits:

- Annual billing gives "a 20% discount", with the full year paid upfront. Midjourney's [How to Subscribe](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/31974654274573-How-to-Subscribe) article says yearly billing is selected by default, so pick the "Monthly Billing" tab before you subscribe if you only want one month.
- Extra Fast time costs **$4 an hour** on every plan.
- Stealth Mode, "which keeps your images and videos private, is only available on the Pro and Mega Plans."
- All plans renew automatically until you set them to cancel.

## The Cheapest Midjourney Plan for Unlimited Images

Standard, at **$30 a month or $24 a month billed yearly**. Midjourney's plan article says: "For unlimited image generations with Relax Mode, subscribe to the Standard, Pro, or Mega Plan." Basic has no Relax time at all, so every Basic image comes out of Fast time: the 200 included minutes plus any extra hours bought at $4 each.

How far 200 minutes goes, using the approximate costs in Midjourney's [GPU Speed article](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32016412137741-GPU-Speed-Fast-Relax-Turbo) (updated 28 August 2026): an SD prompt costs 0.8 minutes and an HD prompt 1.3 minutes.

| Basic plan, 200 Fast minutes | Approximate prompts a month |
|---|---|
| SD prompts at 0.8 minutes | 200 / 0.8 = **250** |
| HD prompts at 1.3 minutes | 200 / 1.3 = **153** |

Those are my arithmetic on Midjourney's approximations; Creative and Subtle upscales, Edit Model prompts and video use more. The trade-off on Standard is speed: Relax prompts "wait in line", with waits Midjourney puts at 0 to 30 minutes. Relax for video is limited to Pro and Mega.

A decision rule from those numbers: if you make a few dozen images a month for posts or decks, Basic covers it. If you iterate on campaign visuals and burn through prompts, Standard removes the meter. If the work must stay private, only Pro and Mega include Stealth Mode.

## Midjourney Price in India: Rupee Conversions and Tax

Midjourney's [Payment Currency article](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27868831424525-Payment-Currency) says it "accepts payments exclusively in US Dollars (USD)". Your bank converts the payment at its own rate and "may also charge a foreign transaction fee". Midjourney publishes no rupee price on the web, so the table below is my conversion, not Midjourney's.

The rate: on 30 October 2026 the ECB euro reference rates were [USD 1.1172](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/eurofxref-graph-usd.en.html) and [INR 106.0840](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/eurofxref-graph-inr.en.html) per euro, which gives **about ₹94.96 per dollar** (106.0840 / 1.1172, rounded to two decimals). The table multiplies by the unrounded ratio and rounds to the nearest rupee.

| Plan | USD monthly | About INR monthly | USD a year | About INR a year |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $10 | ₹950 | $96 | ₹9,116 |
| Standard | $30 | ₹2,849 | $288 | ₹27,347 |

| Mega | $120 | ₹11,395 | $1,152 | ₹1,09,388 |

What gets added on top, per Midjourney's docs:

- **Tax.** The [Tax / VAT Charges](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27868801261325-Tax-VAT-Charges) article says Stripe "calculates your tax rate at checkout using your billing information", adds it to the subscription price, and shows the total before you pay. Billing support cannot change it.
- **Bank charges.** Any foreign transaction fee your card charges, which Midjourney says it does not control.
- **Payment methods.** The [Accepted Payment Methods](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27868831972365-Accepted-Payment-Methods) article names Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards and wallets such as Apple Pay, Amazon Pay, Alipay, Google Pay and Link "in some regions". UPI is not named, and PayPal "isn't currently supported".

The App Store India rupee prices in the niji journey section above are a separate price list, charged by Apple. The app's plans share names with the web tiers, but Midjourney's docs do not say they include the same GPU time, and the rupee prices differ from my web conversions (₹999 against about ₹950 for Basic, ₹2,999 against about ₹2,849 for Standard). My web figures are before the tax Stripe adds at checkout and any card fee, so the gap at checkout is smaller than these numbers suggest. ## Is Midjourney Free for Students?

No. As the route table shows, each student pays for their own plan. What Midjourney's [student billing article](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/42428820154765-Educational-Use-Student-Billing) adds:

- No shared accounts or group plans, and no way for a school to manage student accounts under one subscription.
- "Each account may only have one user, and each user may only have one account."
- A school can reimburse a student, but "the subscription must stay under the student's control".

Schools get no discount either (see the table above). Bulk billing for 50 or more yearly Pro or Mega plans exists, with no discount beyond yearly pricing.

Age matters for younger students. Midjourney's [Terms of Service](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32083055291277-Terms-of-Service) (effective 27 May 2026) require users to be at least 13 and to meet the minimum age of digital consent in their country; below the age to consent, a parent or guardian must agree on their behalf.

## Is Midjourney Free for Commercial Use?

Midjourney's [Terms of Service](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32083055291277-Terms-of-Service) say "You own all Assets You create with the Services to the fullest extent possible under applicable law", and the plan comparison's footnote adds: "If you have subscribed at any point, you are free to use your images/videos in just about any way you want." The [Using Images & Videos Commercially](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27870375276557-Using-Images-Videos-Commercially) article (updated 25 March 2026) says "you own all the images and videos you create, even if you decide to cancel your subscription", with two exceptions, listed first below:

| Situation | What Midjourney's docs say |
|---|---|
| Your company grosses more than $1,000,000 a year | You need a Pro or Mega plan to use your images commercially |
| You upscaled another user's image | It belongs to the original creator; you need their permission |
| You cancelled later | Ownership "persists even if in subsequent months You downgrade or cancel" (Terms of Service) |
| You used the niji journey trial and never subscribed | The Terms' ownership line carries no subscription condition, but a company over $1,000,000 a year must be on Pro or Mega to own its images; no article cited here addresses niji trial images separately |
| Copyright questions | Midjourney "cannot offer guidance on copyright matters" and suggests a lawyer |

The Terms also grant Midjourney a licence to reproduce and publicly display your prompts and images, and say content is "publicly viewable and remixable" by default. Stealth Mode (Pro and Mega only) is a promise to make "best efforts not to publish" your images; the licence grant applies on every plan.

## Try a Paid Plan With the 20-Minute Refund Rule

Without a web trial, the closest thing to a test run is Midjourney's refund rule. Its [Requesting a Refund](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/25386088618253-Requesting-a-Refund) article (updated 17 September 2026) says you are eligible "only if you've used less than 20 GPU minutes throughout the lifetime of your account", counting Fast, Turbo and Relax time.

- At the 0.8-minute SD estimate, 20 minutes is about 25 SD prompts (20 / 0.8, my arithmetic). Stop before that if you might want your money back.
- If you qualify, the refund option appears when you cancel. If it does not appear, Midjourney says it cannot refund.
