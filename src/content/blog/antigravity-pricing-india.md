Google's Antigravity docs give an account without Google AI Pro or Ultra "a meaningful quota, refreshed weekly", and give Google AI Pro "a high, generous quota, refreshed every five hours until the weekly limit is reached". The five-hour rhythm starts at Google AI Pro, **₹1,950 a month** on [Google's India plan page](https://gemini.google/in/subscriptions/?hl=en-IN) when I read it logged out from an Indian connection on 31 October 2026 (Cloudflare's trace returned `loc=IN`). That gap is the whole of Antigravity pricing for an individual in India, because Antigravity has no price of its own: [its pricing page](https://antigravity.google/pricing) lists a $0 plan and then points to Google's AI plans.

Google AI Plus at ₹399 is the cheaper paid plan, but Antigravity's docs give the five-hour refresh only to AI Pro and Ultra, so Plus falls in the weekly group with no plan at all, and for Antigravity the real choice is between ₹0 and ₹1,950. Here is the Google Antigravity price in India for every plan, as read on 31 October 2026:

|---|---|---|---|
| No plan (free) | ₹0 | "Basic weekly rate limits" (Developer tab) | Weekly |
| Google AI Plus | ₹399 | None | Weekly (not Pro or Ultra) |
| Google AI Pro | ₹1,950 | "Entry rate limits to agent model in Google Antigravity" | Every five hours, up to a weekly limit |

Prices and wording as read on 31 October 2026. The India plan page prints INR with no tax line and no yearly price. Google last changed Antigravity's plans on 19 May 2026, and its docs say "Usage limits for this service are subject to modification."

## Is Antigravity Free?

Yes: [Antigravity's pricing page](https://antigravity.google/pricing) lists a $0 plan for individuals, "without a subscription plan", with "Basic weekly rate limits", "Unlimited Tab completions" and "Unlimited Command requests". Google's India plan page lists the same free tier under its Developer tab as "No cost to start".

What every plan gets, free included, per the "Baseline quota" section of [Antigravity's plans docs](https://antigravity.google/docs/plans) on 31 October 2026:

- Gemini models as the core agent model.
- Unlimited Tab completions.
- "Access to all product features, such as scheduled tasks and the CLI".

What changes with a paid plan is how much agent work you get and how fast it comes back. The docs say the limits "correlate with the amount of work done by the agent", so straightforward tasks leave room for more prompts and complex tasks use more quota. None of the Google pages I read gives a prompt or token count for the free tier limits or any paid tier.

### Is Antigravity Free for Students in India?

Google's free student plan in India is Google AI Plus, not AI Pro, and [Google's India student page](https://gemini.google/in/students/?hl=en-IN) does not mention Antigravity.

For Antigravity, that plan sits in the weekly group: the docs grant the five-hour refresh only to AI Pro and Ultra, and [Google One's India plans page](https://one.google.com/intl/en_in/about/google-ai-plans/) rates AI Plus "Limited" on Antigravity agent requests. The same Google One page also offers students an upgrade "to get AI Pro and YouTube Premium" for "a savings of up to 70%", with SheerID verification, but shows no rupee price to a logged-out visitor. That discounted AI Pro is the student route that would bring the five-hour refresh. [My guide to Google's Gemini student offer](/blog/gemini-student-offer/) covers who qualifies and how to claim.

### Is Antigravity Free for Jio Users?

Not for new claims. On 31 October 2026, [Jio's terms page](https://www.jio.com/jcms/en-in/jio-google-gemini-terms-and-conditions/) read "The Google Gemini Offer Is No Longer Available", after the offer period ended on 29 October 2026.

If you claimed in time, Jio's terms, as I read them on 16 October 2026 for my Jio guide, defined your subscription as Google's plan, "named Google AI Pro or such other name as determined by Google", and Antigravity's docs assign quota by Google AI plan, so a claimed Jio plan should count as AI Pro in Antigravity until your 18 months end. Neither Google nor Jio says so in those words.

On Google's AI Developers forum, two users have reported problems in practice. A [thread of 28 September 2026](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/subject-jio-google-ai-pro-entitlement-not-recognized-by-antigravity/185392) says an account whose Google One settings showed AI Pro offered through Jio stopped being accepted by Antigravity on 25 September 2026, and a [thread of 1 October 2026](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/google-ai-pro-provided-by-jio-entitlement-not-provisioned-for-antigravity-403-subscription-required-3501-while-code-assist-standard-tier-is-granted/186257) says "Antigravity treats the account as free tier"; that poster's Google account was registered in Germany, and a reply in the thread put the problem down to the plan's Indian region not matching the account's. These are user reports, not a statement from Google. The [Jio Gemini Pro offer guide](/blog/jio-gemini-pro-offer/) covers when your free months end and what month 19 costs.

## Antigravity Pro vs Ultra: What 5x and 20x Buy

Ultra's two prices buy a multiple of AI Pro's rate limit on Gemini models. [Antigravity's plan update of 19 May 2026](https://antigravity.google/blog/changes-to-antigravity-plans) says "the $100/month Ultra plan has a rate limit of 5X worth of tokens compared to the $20/month Pro plan", and the $200 plan 20X. The same post added the $100 tier and cut the top Ultra plan from $250 to $200 a month.

Per unit of AI Pro's rate limit, in rupees (my arithmetic on Google's India prices and the multipliers in that post; the multipliers cover Gemini models, and Google says non-Gemini models "remain on a separate fixed rate limit"):

| Plan | ₹ a month | Rate limit vs AI Pro | ₹ per AI Pro's worth of rate limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google AI Pro | ₹1,950 | 1x | ₹1,950 |
| Google AI Ultra 5x | ₹6,500 | 5x | **₹1,300** |
| Google AI Ultra 20x | ₹19,500 | 20x | **₹975** |

Two more points come from Antigravity's docs:

- Models: [Antigravity's Models page](https://antigravity.google/docs/models/) (31 October 2026) lists Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Claude Opus 5.5 on paid Google AI Pro and on Ultra, but not on a Pro trial, and Gemini 4 Argon on Pro and Ultra only. The free tier and AI Plus get Gemini 3.8 Flash, plus Claude Sonnet 4.6, Claude Opus 4.6 and GPT-OSS-120b, which the page says will be removed on 2 November 2026.
- The weekly limit: per [Antigravity's plans docs](https://antigravity.google/docs/plans), Pro's five-hour refresh runs "until the weekly limit is reached", and Ultra gets "the highest weekly rate limits". A heavy week can still use up a Pro plan before its weekly limit resets.

If you reach AI Pro's weekly limit before the week resets, Ultra 5x costs ₹4,550 more a month for five times the rate limit on Gemini models; the alternative is staying on Pro and buying AI credits for the rest of that week.

## What Happens When You Run Out of Antigravity Quota?

It depends on your plan. On the free tier and AI Plus, you wait for the weekly refresh: the docs offer AI credits for overage only to "Users on Google AI Pro or Ultra plans", and [Google One Help](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/17103110?hl=en-IN) says "AI credits are not available for purchase through Google AI Plus plans."

On AI Pro or Ultra, the docs give you an "AI Credit Overages" setting with two options:

| Setting | What Antigravity does when a model's baseline quota runs out |
|---|---|
| Never | "wait until the baseline quota refreshes before using this model further" |
| Always | Uses AI credits, and "automatically switches back to using the baseline quota once it refreshes" |

In the Antigravity CLI the same choice is an on/off "Use G1 Credits" setting (`useG1Credits` in settings.json), per the [CLI AI credits docs](https://antigravity.google/docs/cli/credits/).

What Google publishes about the credits themselves, as of 31 October 2026:

- Price basis: credits "are consumed at standard Gemini Enterprise consumption pricing", per the [plans docs](https://antigravity.google/docs/plans). Neither the docs nor the pricing page states a per-credit price, and the Google One AI credits page asked me to sign in, so I saw no rupee price for credits.
- Pack sizes: Google One Help gives "2,500, 5,000 or 20,000 AI credits" as examples, with auto-reload once you have bought credits.
- Expiry: "AI credits may expire after a certain period, as set out when you acquire them."
- Family plans: "Members of a family group won't be allowed to purchase AI credits separately"; only the plan manager can.
- Where they apply: the same Help page says Pro and Ultra members can buy credits "to get extra usage in Google Flow and Google Antigravity".

The May 2026 update also changed what credits are for: Google removed AI credits from the base plans, raised the base entitlements for Gemini models on Antigravity, and since then credits "are now used solely as an overage mechanism".

## Antigravity for Teams Through Google Cloud

For organisations, [Antigravity's pricing page](https://antigravity.google/pricing) points to a Business plan "via Google Cloud" instead of Google AI plans. It lists API access "with consumption-based pricing", and for eligible Gemini Enterprise customers "Standard/Plus plans starting at $30 USD per seat per month" or a "Pay-as-you-go plan with $0 seat fee and consumption based pricing". The page gives these prices in US dollars only.

## Antigravity Pricing Comes Down to ₹0 or ₹1,950

Google's docs give the free tier and AI Plus a weekly quota, and AI Pro, at ₹1,950 a month in India, is the lower-priced of the two plans that refresh every five hours and can buy credits. Start free, note the day your weekly quota runs out, and pay for AI Pro only if that day comes before you are done for the week.

Before you pay, reopen [Antigravity's plans docs](https://antigravity.google/docs/plans) and Google's India plan page: Google last changed this Antigravity pricing in May 2026.
