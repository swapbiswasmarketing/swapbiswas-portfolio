Pick ChatGPT when a marketing job ends in an image or needs unlimited free text chat, and pick Claude when a draft needs rethinking rather than rewording. For research, files, connectors and coding, both $20 plans ship comparable features, so the difference between Claude and ChatGPT there comes down to the tools you already use.

Every vendor fact below comes from Anthropic's or OpenAI's own pages as checked on 26 September 2026, after Anthropic released [Claude Opus 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5) on 22 September and OpenAI [temporarily paused new sign-ups and upgrades to its $200 Pro plan](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6950777-what-is-chatgpt-plus) on 10 September.

## What Is the Difference Between Claude and ChatGPT?

Claude is Anthropic's AI assistant and ChatGPT is OpenAI's. Both offer a free plan and a $20-a-month plan with cited web research, file analysis, memory, projects, connectors and a coding agent. The practical differences: ChatGPT generates images and gives free users unlimited text chats on GPT-5.6 Luna, while Claude does not generate photos or illustrations and offers annual billing.

### Claude vs ChatGPT for Writing and Editing Copy

For writing, the evidence I can point to is one same-prompt test. Amanda Caswell at Tom's Guide ran [the same five prompts through ChatGPT-6 and Claude Opus 5.5](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/i-tested-chatgpt-6-vs-claude-opus-5-5-with-5-everyday-prompts-it-wasnt-even-close) on 24 September 2026, and Claude came out ahead in four.

- The writing round asked for a rewrite of a dull two-sentence product announcement that kept every fact and used no em dashes, no cliches and none of "exciting," "revolutionary," "game-changing" or "seamless". Claude won it.
- ChatGPT's rewrite met the brief too: the reviewer says it "avoided AI styles and retained every factual detail." Claude won because it "understood that a 'human' rewrite requires rethinking how to explain the value of the features, rather than just shifting a few verbs in the original text."
- ChatGPT won the fifth prompt, an open-ended request for a personal system. Across the test, the reviewer found ChatGPT "the less 'fluffy' of the two models, with a response that gets to the point faster," and said that became an advantage in the final round.
- Anthropic's [Opus 5.5 announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5) says the model "follows the writing rules you give it." That is the vendor's claim about its own model, and the Tom's Guide round did not test it.

For marketing copy, that points to Claude when a draft needs rethinking, such as turning a feature list into benefits, and to ChatGPT when you want a short, direct first pass. It matches what I wrote in my [free AI tools for marketing roundup](/blog/free-ai-tools-for-marketing/): ChatGPT for speed, Claude for quality. Five prompts from one reviewer will not settle it for your own briefs.

### Research With Citations

Both tools run multi-step web research and show their sources, so this job is close to a tie.

- Claude's Research is on paid plans only, and Anthropic warns that "research sessions can use up your limits faster due to Claude retrieving multiple sources" ([Research help](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11088861-use-research-on-claude)).
- OpenAI's [web search docs](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/web-search) place deep research in ChatGPT Work, the mode you switch to from Chat with a toggle above the message box ([desktop app docs](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/app)). Free gets "Limited ChatGPT Work access on desktop", and Plus gets "Expanded access to ChatGPT Work on desktop, web, and mobile".
- For a long analyst report or a set of interview transcripts, Claude's paid plans support up to a 1M-token context window on the newest models, per Anthropic's [usage and length limits article](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11647753-how-do-usage-and-length-limits-work). OpenAI's pricing table lists a 256K total context window for its reasoning models on Plus and 54K for Instant.

Whichever you pick, open every cited link before a number goes into a deck. On this site every statistic is re-fetched on its cited page before it ships, and a Claude Research or ChatGPT deep research answer deserves the same check.

### Images and Ad Creative

This is the cleanest split. Claude answers a visual brief with a diagram, chart or interactive visual built in HTML and SVG, or with [Claude Design](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-design-anthropic-labs), which launched on 17 April 2026 as a research preview and makes designs, prototypes, slides, one-pagers and marketing collateral. As of September 2026, Anthropic's [Claude Design help article](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14604416-get-started-with-claude-design) says it "is available in beta on Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans." When a Claude Design mockup has to become a page on your own site, its Export menu offers a Handoff to Claude Code, and [my routing of marketing deliverables between the Claude Design canvas and a repo](/blog/claude-design-vs-claude-code/) shows which ones need that step.

ChatGPT's pricing page lists "Limited and slower image generation" on Free and "More complex and accurate image creation" on Plus. For ad variants, social visuals and product mockups, start in ChatGPT.

### Data Analysis and Files

Claude's pricing table marks "Create and edit files with code execution" as available on Free, while ChatGPT's marks file uploads and data analysis as "Limited" on Free. On the $20 plans, both will read a campaign export or a survey CSV, run the analysis and hand back a file. Test with a file you already understand, so you can spot a wrong total.

### Memory and Projects

Both remember context across chats and both group work into projects, even on Free, where ChatGPT's memory is marked "Limited" and Claude caps you at 5 projects. The marketing use is the same in both: put your positioning doc, ICP, messaging house and brand voice rules in one project, so every chat can draw on them. On Claude, only the project instructions are certain to reach every chat, because paid plans search project knowledge instead of loading all of it once it nears the context window. Keep the positioning statement and core tone rules in the instructions and turn the brand-voice review into a skill; [my asset-by-asset split of Claude project instructions, knowledge and skills](/blog/claude-projects-vs-skills/) sorts the rest of a marketing kit. If you use ChatGPT this way, my guide to [using ChatGPT for sales and marketing](/blog/how-to-use-chatgpt-for-sales-and-marketing/) has workflows that plug straight into a project.

### Connectors and MCP

Both connect to outside tools through the Model Context Protocol (MCP), an open standard for linking AI tools to data sources. My own keyword research runs through the Ahrefs and Semrush MCP servers inside Claude Code, so I can confirm MCP works in Claude Code for SEO data. Pick the tool whose connectors match the stack you already run.

## Where Claude Is Better, and Where ChatGPT Is

Claude is better at a short list of things, and ChatGPT has a list of its own.

Claude is ahead when:

- The copy has to explain its value differently for readers. That was the reviewer's reason for Claude's writing win at Tom's Guide, and Anthropic separately says Opus 5.5 follows the writing rules you give it (a vendor claim).
- You want thorough answers to multi-factor questions. Claude won the party-planning, vacation-rental and city-budget rounds, and the reviewer summed it up as "Claude tends to keep digging."
- You will commit for a year. Claude Pro has an annual price; ChatGPT Plus has none.

ChatGPT is ahead when:

- The deliverable is an image, a mockup or an ad visual.
- You want a short, direct answer. ChatGPT won the open-ended round, and the reviewer found it "the less 'fluffy' of the two models."
- You want a cheaper paid tier than Plus. ChatGPT Go, which OpenAI calls "a low-cost subscription plan" ([Go help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11989085-what-is-chatgpt-go)), sits between Free and Plus, and the pricing page says it "may include ads". Claude lists no plan between Free and Pro.
- You live on a free plan. ChatGPT Free lists unlimited text chats with GPT-5.6 Luna, subject to abuse guardrails.

So is Claude better than ChatGPT? For a content marketer rewriting launch copy, the Tom's Guide result points that way. For a performance marketer shipping ad creative every day, no.

The annual math: twelve months of Plus is 12 x $20 = $240, and a year of Claude Pro billed annually is $200, so Claude costs **$40 less** if you commit. Paid monthly, both come to $240 a year.

On Claude, the shared pool means a long Claude Code session eats into your chat allowance. On ChatGPT's desktop app, Codex CLI and IDE extension, built-in image generation "counts toward your general Codex usage limits", per OpenAI's [image generation docs](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/image-generation).

Is Claude Pro worth it over ChatGPT Plus? Pay for Pro if you want Claude Code in the same subscription, rewrite a lot of launch copy, or will commit for a year. Pay for Plus if images are part of your weekly output or you want expanded Codex and deep research.

## How to Test Claude and ChatGPT Before You Pay

Both free plans are enough to run a fair test in an afternoon, before any money changes hands.

1. Pick three briefs you will ship this month, for example a launch email, a positioning statement and a 1,200-word blog section.
2. Write one prompt per brief with hard constraints: word count, audience, banned words and one source file both tools get.
3. Run each prompt on the same day in a Claude incognito chat, which Anthropic says "won't use Claude's existing memory" ([incognito help](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12260368-use-incognito-chats)), and in ChatGPT with memory turned off under Settings > Personalization ([OpenAI docs](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/customization/memories)), so neither draws on stored context.
4. Score the outputs blind. Count broken constraints, check every number against the source file, and time the edits before each draft is shippable.
5. Pay for the tool that needed fewer edits, then spend the first month on the features you bought it for, such as Claude Code or the fuller image creation on ChatGPT Plus.

For the prompts themselves, my [ChatGPT prompts for marketing](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-marketing/) work in both tools with light edits. If you want a checklist for spotting machine-sounding copy while you score, the AI-writing tells catalogued at [tropes.fyi](https://tropes.fyi/tropes-md) are a good start.

## Claude vs ChatGPT for Marketing: Which to Pay For

- **No budget:** use ChatGPT Free for unlimited text chat on GPT-5.6 Luna, and keep Claude Free alongside it for file creation, code execution and one custom connector.
- **PMM doing competitive research:** pick the tool that connects to your sources. Claude Research can read connected Gmail, Calendar and Docs on paid plans, and OpenAI's deep research steps tell you to "include relevant files or connected app context" ([OpenAI docs](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/web-search)).
- **Builder of tools and automations:** either, since both $20 plans include a coding agent. I use Claude Code.
- **Images and copy every week:** both $20 plans, which together cost less than one $100 tier.
