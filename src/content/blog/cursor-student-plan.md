By September 2025 the Cursor student plan was onboarding only students "with .edu email addresses in the USA", as an account labelled Community Developer wrote on [Cursor's forum in September 2025](https://forum.cursor.com/t/student-verifications-outside-usa-and-organizations-without-edu-email/133736), and by May 2025 Indian students were already reporting that [India was missing from the verification country dropdown](https://forum.cursor.com/t/why-is-india-missing-from-the-country-dropdown-for-student-offers-on-cursor-ai/88955). Then the whole student discount closed: Cursor's [student discount help page](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/student-discount) says "Cursor discontinued new sign-ups for the legacy student discount on June 25, 2026."

So by Cursor's own forum, an Indian college email could not unlock the plan from September 2025 at the latest, and since June nobody can (every page below was checked on 31 October 2026). My read of those pages: an Indian student should stop hunting for a verification link and pick from the three routes Cursor still names, which depend on whether you are an undergraduate, a postgraduate or researcher, or someone who only needs Cursor for coursework.

## Does Cursor Have a Student Plan in India?

No. Cursor stopped new sign-ups for its student discount on 25 June 2026, for everyone, and before that its forum said only US .edu addresses qualified. Students who redeemed it earlier keep their rate until the plan expires. Undergraduates can claim offers at Cursor events; postgraduates and researchers can request credits.

What the help page says, quoted from the page as served on 31 October 2026:

| Question on the help page | Cursor's answer |
|---|---|
| Why did Cursor discontinue the student discount? | "The program had become a target for fraud and held Cursor back from reaching students globally." |
| I'm mid-subscription on the student discount. Does my rate hold? | "Yes. If you already redeemed your student discount, you'll keep your current rate until your plan expires." |
| What happens after the student discount year ends? | "Your subscription continues at the regular rate for your plan unless you cancel. On a legacy Pro plan, that's $20/month." |
| How can I get access to Cursor as a student now? | "Undergraduates can claim credits and discounts at on-campus and online events starting this fall. Master's students, PhD candidates, academic researchers, and educators can fill out this form to request credits to support their work." |

If you claimed the discount before 25 June, the third row is the one to diarise: the renewal is automatic unless you cancel.

Two details in that table matter if you read older guides:

- One user in the May 2025 thread wrote that India had been "removed" from the country list and that "some have already claimed" before that. Nobody from Cursor confirmed or denied it in the thread, so whether India was ever on the list is unverified.
- The January 2026 reply links to the September 2025 thread and asks non-US students to post their country and email domain "to help measure demand". The student discount closed five months later without an expansion to India appearing on any Cursor page I checked.

## Cursor Start in India Is Now a Legacy Plan

The /start page still sells the ₹649 Start plan, but Cursor's own help pages say it is for existing subscribers:

- The [Cursor Start help page](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/cursor-start) opens: "Cursor Start is a legacy Cursor plan for developers in India who subscribed before The X Subscription. It keeps working as it is. New customers choose a plan on The X Subscription; the comparable tier is Plus."
- The [legacy Cursor plans page](https://cursor.com/docs/account/pricing/legacy-cursor-plans) lists Hobby, Start, Pro, Pro+, Ultra and Teams as legacy plans and says "New customers start on The X Subscription."
- In the 19 October thread, a free-plan user in India clicked "Upgrade to Start" and landed on "Create Your X Subscription". Cursor staff replied: "Your account isn't being singled out, and there's no separate India checkout you're missing."
- The Cursor Start help page also still says "Start can only be purchased while your account is on the free plan", although its opening line calls Start legacy, and that subscribing to Start involves verifying an Indian phone number.

Cursor's page that explains The X Subscription, [What is The X Subscription?](https://cursor.com/help/the-x-subscription/overview), describes it as "one subscription that brings Grok, Cursor, and X together", with a shared weekly usage pool. It mentions no student price.

## Cursor Student Plan Not Working? Three Causes From Cursor's Pages

If a student verification or a cheaper India plan is failing for you, one of these is the likely reason, each from a page linked above:

1. **The student discount is closed.** New sign-ups ended on 25 June 2026, so there is no verification step left to pass, whatever your email domain.
2. **Before 25 June, your domain did not qualify.** The September 2025 forum post said onboarding was limited to US .edu addresses, and the January 2026 reply named .ac.in domains as unsupported.
3. **"Upgrade to Start" opens a different checkout.** That is the redirect staff described on 19 October 2026: Start is legacy, and new subscriptions go through The X Subscription.

A student offer bought from a reseller is not a fix. The pricing page says: "Subscriptions are only sold directly through X Subscription Corp. We do not authorize any resellers or third-party sellers."

## Which Cursor Route Fits Your Student Type

Each route below quotes Cursor's own page. None of these pages names a country or promises a credit amount.

| You are | Route Cursor names | Where it is stated | What to expect |
|---|---|---|---|
| An undergraduate | "credits and discounts at on-campus and online events starting this fall", plus a campus newsletter for "early access to upcoming events, exclusive discounts" | [Student discount help page](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/student-discount) | No amount, date or list of Indian events is published |
| Any university student who wants to run campus events | Campus Leads, for "a current university student" who can give "5 hours per week"; benefits include "Access to 6 months of Cursor Ultra" | [Campus Leads page](https://cursor.com/campus-leads) | "Applications will open again in January" |
| A master's student, PhD candidate, researcher or educator | A form to "request credits to support their work", titled "Request Credits for Academic Work" | [Student discount help page](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/student-discount), which links the [credits form](https://anysphere.typeform.com/to/bgTJGDeA) | The help page gives no amount or approval time |
| Using Cursor only for coursework | The free tier: "Anyone can get started with Cursor for free" | [Students page](https://cursor.com/students), [pricing page](https://cursor.com/pricing) | Limited agent and chat requests; paid tiers are on The X Subscription |
| Already on the old student discount | Keep "your current rate until your plan expires" | [Student discount help page](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/student-discount) | Renews at the regular rate, $20 a month on legacy Pro, unless you cancel |

If you are a postgraduate, send the credits form first: it is the only route on the list that asks Cursor for credits directly. If you are an undergraduate, subscribe to the campus newsletter and use the free tier until an event offer reaches you.

## Cursor Student Plan: Use the Route You Already Qualify For

The Cursor student plan is closed, and its forum said Indian .ac.in addresses did not qualify even before that. The ₹649 Start plan is legacy for new buyers per Cursor's help page, though /start still advertises it.

The help page promises student "discounts and credits through on-campus and online events this fall", with no date for India yet, so recheck it before you rely on this page. Postgraduates: open [Cursor's credits form](https://anysphere.typeform.com/to/bgTJGDeA) today.
