The rules behind those rows:

- From the third day inside one 7-day stretch, a week pass costs less than day passes: ₹199 against 3 x ₹69 = ₹207.
- For days in a row, passes cost less than the ₹500 monthly plan up to 15 days, where two week passes and a day pass come to ₹467. At 16 days they cost ₹536, and the monthly plan is lower.
- For days scattered across a month, price each 7-day stretch: ₹199 if it holds 3 or more of your days, ₹69 a day if it holds fewer. When no stretch holds 3 of your days, the monthly plan costs less from the eighth day: seven 1-day passes come to ₹483 and eight to ₹552.
- Passes do not renew. A subscription does: Canva's [upgrade help article](https://www.canva.com/help/upgrade-to-canva-pro-or-business/) says "Your plan will auto-renew unless you cancel."
- The same article says a free trial runs on "the same size allowance as Canva Free" for AI, with the plan's full allowance arriving when you pay, so a trial is a poor test of AI-heavy work.
- Neither that article nor the pricing page states how long the trial lasts. The article says the end date shows under Settings > Billing & Teams, and a trial started without payment details ends on its own with no charge.
- If your Canva account and card have never had a free trial, the trial costs ₹0 for a short job that fits Free's AI allowance; the passes are the cheapest route once it is used.

## Canva Pro Day and Week Passes

Canva sells Pro, and no other plan, as a one-day or one-week prepaid pass in India: ₹69 and ₹199. The pricing page's FAQ sets the terms:

- What you get: "With this option, you can make a single upfront payment to access Canva Pro's premium features."
- Renewal: "There are no auto-renewals, so you aren't charged automatically after the prepaid period ends."
- Extending: you can "top-up during the one-day or one-week prepaid plan or re-purchase once the plan ends".
- Plans covered: "The one-time payment option is available for accessing Canva Pro only." Business has no pass.
- AI: the FAQ gives no AI figure for a pass, but Canva's AI Pass page says that on a daily or weekly plan "your monthly AI allowance will be pro-rated", so a pass carries only a share of Pro's monthly allowance. Check your balance in Canva before you plan AI-heavy work around one.

Canva's pages differ on paying for a pass with UPI. The pricing page's FAQ on why the one-time option may not appear lists five methods (credit or debit card, PayPal, GoPay in Indonesia, GCash in the Philippines and PIX in Brazil), and UPI is not one of them. Canva's [UPI help article](https://www.canva.com/en_in/help/pay-via-upi/) says UPI "is only available for one-time payments, such as licences, prints, prepaid credits, or short-duration plans". Keep a card ready in case the pass checkout does not offer UPI.

## Is Canva Pro Free for Students in India?

Canva's own free routes for students run only through a school or university: its free education plan covers school students, and a university student gets premium access through Canva only if the university has bought Canva Campus. A Jio recharge bundle reported in September 2026 has its own section below.

- Canva Education is free, and Canva's [eligibility guidelines](https://www.canva.com/en_in/education/eligibility-guidelines/) say it is "currently available for K-12 (primary or secondary) teachers and institutions" and "not currently available for higher education teachers and institutions (colleges and universities)".
- A school student joins through a teacher. The pricing page's FAQ says "your teacher needs to invite you to join Canva Education."
- Canva Campus is bought by the institution. The FAQ says it "is based on Canva Enterprise pricing", that institutions must buy a minimum number of staff licences, and that "all students at the university receive premium Canva access at no additional cost" once that threshold is met.
- Other students get the standard plans. In the FAQ's words: "If you're a student at a Canva Campus institution, you can simply log-in with your university email, however if not you can still access Canva Free or Pro at log-in."
- The Education tab lists three offers, Teachers, Schools and Districts, and Campus, and none of them is a discounted plan a student buys alone.

A college student comparing design tools has one route Canva lacks: Adobe sells a student plan in India that you can buy on your own, with a lower first-year price that rises at renewal, and [the two-year cost of Adobe's India student plan](/blog/adobe-student-plan-india/) is the figure to set against Canva Pro. For UI or product design coursework, Figma's help article gives verified students its Professional plan free, and [how long Figma's student access lasts](/blog/figma-student-plan/) depends on whether you study at a school, a college or a bootcamp.

| Method | What Canva's help says |
|---|---|
| UPI | "only available for one-time payments, such as licences, prints, prepaid credits, or short-duration plans"; on desktop you scan a QR code and approve the payment in your UPI app |
| UPI Autopay | For "Canva Pro or Canva Pro Lite only"; "UPI Autopay isn't available on Canva Business plan"; "UPI Autopay only supports payments up to 15,000 INR" |

From the [UPI Autopay article](https://www.canva.com/en_in/help/pay-upi-autopay/): you approve a mandate in your UPI app, "Your bank or UPI app will send you a notification before each recurring charge", and you cancel the mandate from your UPI app or your bank's settings. Every Pro and Pro Lite price above sits under the ₹15,000 limit. Canva's [supported payment options page](https://www.canva.com/en_in/help/payment-options/) lists "UPI and UPI Autopay (India)" beside cards, PayPal and Google Pay.

## Does Canva's Price in India Include GST?

Yes, by Canva's tax help: its [general sales tax article](https://www.canva.com/en_in/help/general-sales-tax/) says that outside the United States and Canada, "Canva's advertised price includes sales tax". The pricing page's plan cards carry no tax wording of their own.

For a business in India, Canva's [sales tax by country article](https://www.canva.com/en_in/help/canva-sales-tax-per-country/) has two sentences on India:

- Print orders carry GST for every buyer: "Canva is required to charge GST on any supplies of printed products, including for business customers."
- Subscriptions can be invoiced without it: "if you're a business customer and purchase a subscription, there will be no GST on your invoice if you provide a valid GSTIN at the time of purchase."
- Neither sentence says whether the amount you are charged changes when GST comes off the invoice.

Canva's [tax-exempt invoice article](https://www.canva.com/en_in/help/tax-exempt-invoice/) adds that exemption requests "can only be submitted via the Canva website" and not in the mobile app, and that an exemption applies only to invoices issued after you submit your details. Canva says it "can't offer tax advice", so take the GSTIN question to your accountant.
