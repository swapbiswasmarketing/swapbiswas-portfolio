"We do not offer standard discounted pricing for any of our paid plans, including Pro plans," says Anthropic's [Pro plan help article](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8325606-what-is-the-pro-plan). For a student in India, that is the direct answer to "Claude student discount India": no individual plan carries a student price. My advice is to stop hunting for a promo code and ask the person who can sign: someone in the office that buys software for your institution, or the faculty member who runs your research group.

Anthropic lists no student price for Claude Pro or Max, in India or anywhere else, as of 10 October 2026. A student in India can use the Free plan, pay the standard price with the annual billing discount, or get Claude through an institution or research group that provides it.

## Does Claude Have a Student Discount?

No, not for an individual. As of 10 October 2026, none of the Anthropic pages linked here shows a student price, a student verification step or a student discount code for a Claude plan.

The sentence this page opens with sits in the Pro article under the heading "How can I get a free or discounted Pro plan?" (the page's metadata dates its last change to 22 September 2026), and the [Max plan article](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11049741-what-is-the-max-plan) repeats it for Max 5x and Max 20x. Both add two limits: "We occasionally run limited-time promotions, but we don't have any standing discounts available upon request", and "Our Support team cannot issue one-off discounts or coupons."

Anthropic does use the word discount on the pages below, and on none of them is it a student-only price on an individual plan:

| Where | Anthropic's wording | Who gets it |
|---|---|---|
| Pro on annual billing | "annual subscription discount", on the pricing page | Anyone who pays for a year up front |
| Education plan | "Comprehensive access for all university members at discounted rates", on the pricing page | An institution that buys the plan |
| Team plan for scientists | "this discounted Claude Team plan", in the program page's FAQ | A verified principal investigator's research group |
| Usage bundles | "Larger bundles come with bigger discounts" ([Buy usage bundles](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14246112-buy-usage-bundles)) | Pro, Max and Team subscribers who prepay for usage credits |
| Artifact usage promotion | After Claude creates or edits an artifact, "a portion of the work that follows uses 50% less of your five-hour session limit", and "After the discount period ends, messages count at the standard rate", from 1 to 15 October 2026 ([promotion article](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17274727-artifact-usage-promotion)) | Pro, Max and Team plans, and Free plans from 7 October |

The last row is the promotion that was running on the day I checked: it is published in the help center, it has an end date, and its FAQ says "There's no code or setting to turn on."

### Claude Pro Student Discount in India: Standard Price, Annual Billing Only

- **The price.** The Pro article says the plan costs "$20 per month (US), with pricing in your local currency where supported", adds that "Annual subscriptions are billed once yearly at a discounted rate", and tells you to "Visit claude.ai/upgrade or check your mobile app store for current pricing in your region." The Claude Pro price breakdown linked in the route table has the rupee figures by checkout, the annual-plan arithmetic and Claude Pro against ChatGPT Go in India.
- **Promotions.** Anthropic's [coupon article](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11107691-why-is-a-coupon-or-promotion-not-available-for-my-account), dated 16 March 2026, says "Coupons and promotions are generally only available to users trying out a paid plan for the first time." It adds that App Store subscribers on iOS or Android "are unable to take advantage of promotions at this time". By that article, a coupon or promotion generally needs a first paid subscription bought directly, outside the app stores. The Artifact usage promotion of 1 to 15 October 2026 works differently: its article says "If you're on an eligible plan, the discount applies to your account automatically." OpenAI's own India promotion, a free year of ChatGPT Go, no longer takes new sign-ups, and if you redeemed it, [what you are charged when that free year ends](/blog/chatgpt-go-free-india/) depends on where you signed up.
- **Claude Code.** The pricing FAQ says "Claude Code is included in all paid plans", so there is no separate Claude Code price to discount. What else each plan includes is in [Claude vs Claude Code, plan by plan](/blog/claude-vs-claude-code/).

If the choice is Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus, [Claude and ChatGPT compared job by job, with Pro against Plus](/blog/claude-vs-chatgpt/) covers it.

## Is Claude Free for Students Through GitHub?

Not as a Claude subscription, and not as a model you can pick. GitHub gives verified students its own Copilot Student plan at no charge, and as of 10 October 2026 that plan offers auto model selection only. GitHub's notices, in date order:

| Date | What GitHub published |
|---|---|
| 12 March 2026 | Students moved to "a new GitHub Copilot Student plan", and "some premium models, including GPT-5.3-Codex, GPT-5.4, and Claude Opus and Sonnet models, will no longer be available for self-selection" ([GitHub's announcement](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/189268)) |
| 24 June 2026 | "Copilot Free and Student plans will now use Copilot auto model selection as the default and only model selection experience." ([GitHub changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-06-24-changes-to-model-selection-for-free-and-student-plans/)) |
| 10 October 2026, as read | The plans page lists Copilot Student as "Free", with "An allowance of GitHub AI Credits", "Excludes third-party agents" and "Auto model selection only" |

What that leaves a student who wants Claude:

- Auto may route a prompt to a Claude model. GitHub's 12 March notice said that through Auto mode students "continue to have access to a powerful set of models from providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google". Its [auto model selection docs](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/models/auto-model-selection) say Auto "chooses from supported models, subject to your policies and subscription type", and the supported-models table they link to includes several Claude models.
- The Claude coding agent is excluded. GitHub's [Anthropic Claude agent page](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/agents/anthropic-claude) says "Anthropic Claude is available in paid Copilot plans", and the plans page marks third-party agents as not included on Copilot Student.
- The Student Developer Pack has no Anthropic offer. On the [pack page](https://education.github.com/pack), the word Claude appears once, inside another vendor's offer that names Claude Code as a client.
- Eligibility is broad. GitHub's [student application page](https://docs.github.com/en/education/about-github-education/github-education-for-students/apply-to-github-education-as-a-student) asks that you are "enrolled in a degree- or diploma-granting program", can prove current student status and are "at least 13 years old".
- Activation can lag approval. The [student setup page](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/how-tos/copilot-on-github/set-up-copilot/enable-copilot/set-up-for-students) warns: "If only paid options appear, do not complete a purchase. Wait a few days and try again."

Copilot Student is worth claiming if you write code. If Gemini would do instead of Claude, my guide to the [free Google AI Plus routes for college students in India](/blog/internshala-google-ai-plus/) compares Internshala's offer with Google's own one-year student plan. That Google plan is free for 12 months once SheerID verifies you as a student, and it has to be redeemed by 31 December 2026; my [guide to claiming the Gemini student year](/blog/gemini-student-offer/) covers the steps and what changes in month 13.

## Claude Student Discount India: Ask the Person Who Can Sign

The "Who has to act" column of the route table names two parties you can email: whoever buys software at your institution, and your principal investigator. A short message to each is where I would start.

**To your institution's IT office, library or dean's office:**

```text
Subject: Does [institution] have Claude for Education?

Anthropic sells institutions Claude for Education, a plan that covers
students, faculty and staff: https://claude.com/solutions/education

1. Does [institution] already have this plan, or a Claude Enterprise agreement?
2. If not, could [office] ask for a quote through "Contact our Education team"
   on that page?
```

**To your principal investigator, if you work in a research group:**

```text
Subject: Could you apply for Anthropic's Team plan for scientists?

Anthropic's Claude Team plan for scientists gives a verified principal
investigator free Standard seats for the group for 12 months:
https://claude.com/programs/team-plan-for-scientists

Would you apply as PI and add me as a group member?
```

For everything that depends on you alone:

- Use the Free plan until its limits stop your work more than once a week.
- If you write code, apply to GitHub Education and activate Copilot Student.
- If you pay, choose the checkout first. The Claude Pro price breakdown linked in the route table sets the App Store, Google Play and web checkouts side by side, and they do not show one price. Anthropic's coupon article also says App Store subscribers cannot use promotions at this time.
- If you want to lead a campus program, recheck the Campus page in August: its application dates, September 1 to September 12, carry no year.

Until Anthropic publishes a student price, treat any page that promises a Claude student discount Indian students can redeem as unverified, and check it against the Pro article, the pricing page, the education page and the Campus page. Then send the email to your institution.
