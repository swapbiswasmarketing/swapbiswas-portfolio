Over two years, the Adobe student plan India price comes to **₹13,176.00** for a student who pays monthly: ₹398.99 a month in year one and ₹699.01 a month in year two, as [Adobe's India offer terms](https://www.adobe.com/in/offer-terms/ccm-ste-introductory.html) listed them on 31 October 2026. The ₹398.99 that headlines [Adobe's India students page](https://www.adobe.com/in/creativecloud/buy/students.html) covers ₹4,787.88 of that total, a little over a third.

Judge this plan by its second year. Even at the renewal rate, the full student bundle costs less over two years than Photoshop on its own, and if your college joins Adobe's free higher-education offer, you pay nothing for Adobe's Creative Cloud Pro India for Higher Education licence, which Adobe says includes Photoshop, Premiere, Illustrator, Firefly and Acrobat.

I read Adobe's India pages logged out, in a fresh browser profile on an Indian connection, on 31 October 2026. I did not sign in, start a trial or reach a checkout. ## Adobe Student Plan India Price, Checked 31 October 2026

Creative Cloud Pro for students and teachers costs **₹398.99 a month incl. GST** for the first year on an annual plan billed monthly, then **₹699.01 a month**. It is for students and teachers aged 18 or older at an accredited institution, new subscribers only, and it starts with a 7-day free trial before the first charge.

The offer terms give both billing options and the renewal rate Adobe lists today:

| Student plan (Creative Cloud Pro) | Billed monthly | Prepaid yearly |
|---|---|---|
| First year | ₹398.99/mo incl. GST | ₹3,999.00/yr incl. GST |
| After the first year (current rate) | ₹699.01/mo incl. GST | ₹7,005.96/yr incl. GST |

The conditions that sit around those prices, in the terms' own words:

- "A 12-month commitment is required."
- "This pricing is limited to one purchase of one subscription per customer."
- At the end of the offer term the subscription "will automatically renew and your payment method will be charged the then-current rate", and the terms add "Price subject to change at renewal."
- The offer is for "new subscribers" who buy directly from Adobe or through Customer Support, and the terms state no end date for the introductory price.

The [Students and Teachers tab of Adobe's India plans page](https://www.adobe.com/in/creativecloud/plans.html?plan=edu) repeats the pair: "Pay ₹398.99/mo incl. GST the first year and ₹699.01/mo incl. GST after that." The same page opens with "you can cancel within 14 days for a full refund." Prices and offers change, so read the terms again on the day you pay.

## What the Student Plan Includes

[Adobe's students page](https://www.adobe.com/in/creativecloud/buy/students.html) lists the plan as Creative Cloud Pro with the same app lineup as the full Creative Cloud Pro plan. Its FAQ names these apps, among others:

| Use | Apps on the list |
|---|---|
| Photo and design | Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Lightroom, Fresco, Bridge |
| Video and audio | Premiere, After Effects, Audition, Character Animator, Media Encoder |
| Documents and web | Acrobat Pro, Dreamweaver, InCopy, Scan |
| AI and quick design | Adobe Firefly, Adobe Express (Premium) |

On top of the apps, the same page lists **1,000 monthly generative credits** for premium AI video and audio features, unlimited standard AI image features such as Generative Fill, Adobe Fonts, Frame.io for Creative Cloud and **100GB of cloud storage**.

The same page sets two usage rules:

- "An individual Adobe Creative Cloud membership lets you install apps on more than one computer and sign in on up to two computers. However, you can only actively use the apps on one computer at a time."
- Desktop apps run on Mac and Windows; the page says they "are not natively supported on Chromebooks", where only browser tools such as Firefly and Express work.

Adobe's [Education FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/in/x-productkb/policy-pricing/education-faq.html) adds that Student and Teacher Edition products "can be used commercially on your privately owned computer", which matters if you freelance while you study. Creative Cloud members may also qualify for Adobe's LinkedIn Premium benefit; I covered its conditions in [the free LinkedIn Premium routes for India](/blog/how-to-get-linkedin-premium-free/).

Two of those pairs pull against each other, and Adobe does not reconcile either:

- **Age.** The India terms and students page say 18; the help-centre FAQ says 13, and its accreditation footnote names US and Canadian bodies. The 18-or-older line is in the terms attached to this India price, so I would plan on 18.
- **Payment.** The students page says credit card; the plans page shows a UPI logo beside the card logos. Neither page says whether UPI works for the student plan specifically, and I did not reach a checkout to find out. Gizbot's report of the student Photoshop offer describes a UPI AutoPay checkout, which is a different plan, so it does not settle the question for Creative Cloud Pro.

## What Happens to the Adobe Student Plan After One Year?

The plan renews by itself at the end of the first year, at ₹699.01 a month or ₹7,005.96 a year at today's listed rate. The students page FAQ says the second-year price applies "so long as you remain eligible (price subject to change at renewal)".

What happens when you stop being a student comes from the [Education FAQ on Adobe's help centre](https://helpx.adobe.com/in/x-productkb/policy-pricing/education-faq.html) (last updated 28 April 2026):

- "after student graduates from school, students receive a grace year on the education price."
- "After this year is over, the subscription is upgraded to a higher non-education price."
- "Graduated students will receive an email notification about the upcoming price increase 30 (thirty) days before the contract renewal date."

None of the India pages I read names the rupee price a graduate moves to. The nearest published figure is the current renewal rate for Creative Cloud Pro for Individuals, ₹2,714 a month, but Adobe does not say that is the plan a graduate lands on. Put the renewal date in your calendar either way: the terms require a 12-month commitment and the plan renews by itself.

## Is Adobe Free for Students in India?

Not on Adobe's own pages as a plan a student can sign up for alone: Adobe's free Creative Cloud Pro for Indian higher education is claimed by an institution, and an individual student gets a 7-day free trial before the paid plan starts. A free first year of Photoshop for students has been reported by the press but was not on Adobe's Photoshop plans page on 31 October 2026. Four free or low-cost routes are easy to confuse, and only three of them appear on Adobe's own pages.

| Route | Who claims it | Price | Status on 31 October 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free Creative Cloud Pro for Indian higher education | The institution, by email to Adobe | "No charge" | Live on Adobe's page, no dates stated |
| Paid student plan with a 7-day trial | You | ₹398.99/mo, then ₹699.01/mo | Live |
| Free first year of Photoshop for students | You, at checkout | ₹0 for a year, then ₹1,999/yr (press-reported) | Reported by Gizbot on 5 October 2026; not shown on Adobe's Photoshop plans page that day |
| Adobe Express Free | You | ₹0.00 | Live, "No credit card required" |

**The higher-education offer.** [Adobe's India higher-education page](https://www.adobe.com/in/creativecloud/buy/education/india-higher-education-offer.html) says "Students get Photoshop, Premiere, Illustrator, Firefly, Acrobat, and more for free" and "No charge. Institutional verification required for eligibility." An institution qualifies if it is constituted under Indian law, accredited by a competent government or statutory authority, and passes Adobe's verification. It must have Federated ID set up and email hedindiaedu@adobe.com from its registered institutional address. To the question "Can students or faculty request access directly?", the page answers: "Participating institutions will communicate the necessary information directly to faculty and students." If you want it, the person to ask is your college's IT or administration office.

Adobe announced the programme on 19 February 2026 at the India AI Impact Summit ([Adobe's release](https://news.adobe.com/en/apac/news/2026/02/adobe-introduces-photoshop-acrobat-and-firefly-ai-student-offering-for-free-in-india-to-accelerate-vibrant-creator-economy)), putting Firefly, Photoshop and Acrobat "into the hands of students for free, via accredited higher education institutions across India". The release names no plan; the higher-education page names Creative Cloud Pro and lists the apps. Both route access through the institution.

**The free Photoshop year.** [Gizbot reported on 5 October 2026](https://www.gizbot.com/apps/news/get-adobe-photoshop-free-for-one-year-in-india-for-students-how-to-claim-it-128947.html) a promotion giving eligible college students an annual prepaid Photoshop plan at ₹0 for the first year, renewing at ₹1,999 a year, set up through a UPI AutoPay mandate, and wrote that the promotion "may end without notice". [Adobe's India Photoshop plans page](https://www.adobe.com/in/products/photoshop/plans.html) showed Photoshop at ₹599.00 a month and no free Photoshop year for students when I read it on 31 October 2026. If the ₹0 price appears at your checkout, the renewal date is the line to check.

**The free tier.** [Adobe Express Free](https://www.adobe.com/in/express/pricing) is ₹0.00 with 5GB of storage, and Adobe's Photoshop plans FAQ says "Photoshop on mobile is a free mobile version of Photoshop with premium features that are available when you purchase a subscription."

## Budget the Second Year Before the Trial Starts

The Adobe student plan India price climbs in year two: ₹8,388.12 against ₹4,787.88 on monthly billing, and that second-year bill is the number to plan around. Even with it, the bundle still costs less over two years than Photoshop alone. Before you start the 7-day trial, ask your college office whether it has joined Adobe's free higher-education offer.
