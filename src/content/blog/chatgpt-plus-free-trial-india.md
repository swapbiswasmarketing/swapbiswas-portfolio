## Does the ChatGPT Plus Free Trial Renew Automatically?

Yes. OpenAI's trial FAQ says: "ChatGPT will automatically renew as a paid subscription at the end of the initial free trial or promotional subscription period using the payment method on file, unless you cancel before the trial or promotional period ends."

What a renewed Plus subscription costs and how it bills:

- **₹1,999 a month** on the web in India, GST included, on 16 October 2026 per the plan-by-plan table in the rupee price guide, and still ₹1,999 in the India price file named above on 31 October 2026.
- Monthly only. The Plus article says OpenAI does not support "annual billing or the option to pay for multiple months in advance".
- Charged to the payment method you added to claim the trial. Gizbot's October story tells readers to cancel "the subscription or automatic renewal mandate before the free period ends".

### How to Cancel Before the Free Month Renews

OpenAI's [cancel article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7232927-canceling-your-chatgpt-subscription) says to cancel "through the platform where you purchased it" and to "Cancel at least 24 hours before your next billing date to avoid being charged for the next subscription period." If you paid with UPI, the mandate steps for Google Pay, PhonePe and Paytm are in the cancel section of the Go renewal guide linked above.

What you keep after cancelling depends on the offer, and no OpenAI page covers the India one. The Plus promotions article says "If it was a free promotion, your access may end immediately upon cancellation", while the Back to School article says "your Plus access continues through the end of the current monthly billing period" and any remaining free months are forfeited. For when to cancel, see the same cancel section of the Go renewal guide.

## ChatGPT Plus Free Trial Payment Was Not Approved: What OpenAI's Help Says

None of the OpenAI billing help articles I read on 31 October 2026 prints the wording "payment was not approved": I read the titles in its Account, login and billing collection and the two payment articles below. The closest article is [Why was my credit card declined?](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7232916-why-was-my-credit-card-declined), and it starts from one fact: "We typically don't receive detailed decline reasons from banks, so reaching out to them directly is often the quickest way to resolve payment issues."

| Likely cause, per OpenAI | What OpenAI says to do |
|---|---|
| Wrong card details | Check the "card number, expiration date, CVC, and billing address (including zip/postal code)" |
| Not enough money on the card | Make sure the card has "sufficient funds" |
| The bank blocks the payment type | "Some banks block certain online or international transactions by default"; contact your bank or card provider |
| OTP or 3D Secure step failed | Allow pop-ups, turn off ad blockers and VPNs, try an incognito window, "complete the bank's OTP/app approval without closing or refreshing the checkout", and ask the bank to enable 3DS/SCA for online, recurring or international payments |
| Too many subscriptions on one card or device | The [card and device limits article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001559-understanding-card-and-device-limits-in-chatgpt) says a checkout reading "Too many subscriptions associated with this card or device" is "different from a bank decline"; contact Support if you think the restriction is wrong |
| Card issued outside a supported region | "Your payment card must be issued by a bank located in one of those supported regions." |
| A renewal payment failed | The [renewal troubleshooting article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7242622-troubleshooting-chatgpt-plus-or-pro-renewal-payments) says to clear cache and cookies, call the bank and confirm "both you and your card issuer are located in a supported country" |

Three more points for an Indian checkout:

- UPI is listed for Plus. OpenAI's [multi-currency billing article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10421635-multicurrency-billing) says "UPI is available for Go and Plus plans." Neither payment article above mentions UPI, and Gizbot reported that on the free-month offer UPI "may not work for every user", so a card or net banking, the two options Gizbot names, are the others to try.
- If the payment step fails while you activate a promotion, the Plus promotions article says to "try another card accepted for the offer", and warns that a "failed charge can sometimes succeed on a later retry, resulting in a charge even if the first attempt failed."
- When you contact support, the decline article asks for the product, where the error appeared (adding the card, checkout or renewal), "the exact error, and the local time and time zone", and no card number or security code.
