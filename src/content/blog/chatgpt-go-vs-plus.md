ChatGPT Plus costs **2.5 times** as much as ChatGPT Go in the US, $20 a month against $8, on OpenAI's own pages on 16 October 2026. The extra $12 buys the rows of OpenAI's pricing table where Go says No or Limited and Plus says Yes, such as GPT-6 Sol, deep research and Sites, plus an account with no ads. Compared row by row, ChatGPT Go vs Plus comes down to **23 of that table's 86 rows**.

I would choose between the two by the rows a normal marketing week hits, not by the $12. Briefs, emails, posts and rewrites run on GPT-6 Luna in Go and on GPT-6 Sol in Plus, per OpenAI's 7 October 2026 release note, with the same context windows on both, so Go covers them when Luna's drafts need only light edits. Plus earns the extra money in a week that needs a sourced brief from deep research, a deck or spreadsheet built through the Office and Sheets extensions, a page published on Sites or an ad visual from image generation with Thinking.

OpenAI's pages also contradict each other on the questions a Go buyer asks, including ads and voice limits, so I set out both sides of each below and say which one to go with. Seven of the ten OpenAI help articles I cite had been edited in the four days before I read them.

## What Is the Difference Between ChatGPT Go and Plus?

On 16 October 2026, ChatGPT Go costs $8 a month in the US and Plus $20. Both list unlimited text chats and identical context windows. Plus adds GPT-6 Sol in chat (Go: GPT-6 Luna), GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Astra, Sites, image generation with Thinking, fast responses and no ads, and lifts Go's Limited Codex, deep research and Office and Sheets extensions.

The prices come from three OpenAI pages: the [Go launch post](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-go/) ("In the US, Go is available for $8 per month"), the [Plus help article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6950777-what-is-chatgpt-plus) ("Price: $20/month (billed monthly)") and OpenAI's [ChatGPT Work and Codex pricing page](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing), which lists Go at $8, Plus at $20 and Pro "Plans at $100, $200, or $500 USD per month". Every row below is from [OpenAI's pricing page](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) as it was served on 16 October 2026, with Free shown for reference.

### Same on Go and Plus

The other 63 rows match, and 32 of those read No on both plans, 27 of them under the table's "Security & Administration" and "Customer Service" headings. The ones a marketer is likely to care about:

- Everyday text chats, marked "Unlimited\*" on all four plans, and the GPT-6 Luna row, though Plus chats run on GPT-6 Sol

- Model training on your chats, with "Opt-out available" on every plan

What the deciding rows do, in OpenAI's words:

- Images with thinking "can plan and refine image outputs before generating them", per the 21 April 2026 release note. Go's pricing card promises "More image creation"; the Plus card promises "More complex and accurate image creation".
- Sites "lets you build, host, and share a website by describing it in ChatGPT or Codex" and can also "host web apps, games, or a compatible project you've already built", per OpenAI's [Sites documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/sites). OpenAI's [Sites help article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001339-creating-and-using-chatgpt-sites) says it "is available in public beta for ChatGPT workspaces, Plus, and Pro accounts".
- Codex on Go is "GPT-6 Luna at Standard speed in the desktop app, subject to rollout", per the Work and Codex pricing page, while Plus gets "Codex on the web, in the CLI, in the IDE extension, and on iOS" with GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Luna.
- Record mode "can transcribe and summarize audio recordings like meetings, brainstorms, or voice notes", and OpenAI's [ChatGPT record article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11487532-chatgpt-record) says it runs only in the macOS desktop app.
- On ads, the ads FAQ says the system weighs "the context and intent of your current conversation" when it picks an ad, and that OpenAI does "not share your conversations with ChatGPT with advertisers". For client work on Go, the practical issue is a sponsored unit matched to the chat's topic showing under a response on a shared screen. Temporary Chats show no ads.

The text-only jobs are where Go holds up. The ten workflows in [my guide to using ChatGPT for sales and marketing](/blog/how-to-use-chatgpt-for-sales-and-marketing/), such as cold-email personalization and battlecard drafts, are chats with pasted or uploaded context, and Go marks both everyday text chats and file uploads as included.

## ChatGPT Plus vs Pro: Pro 100, Pro 200 and Pro 500 (Checked 16 October 2026)

ChatGPT Pro is three monthly plans, Pro 100 at $100, Pro 200 at $200 and Pro 500 at $500, per OpenAI's [Pro tiers article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-about-chatgpt-pro-tiers), against $20 for Plus. The pricing table differs between Plus and Pro on 19 of its 86 rows, but it shows Pro as one column, so this table splits the tiers using OpenAI's other pages and one news report.

| | Plus | Pro 100 | Pro 200 | Pro 500 | Who says so |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly price, US | $20 | $100 | $200 | $500 | OpenAI's Plus and Pro tiers articles |
| Price as a multiple of Plus | 1x | 5x | 10x | 25x | My arithmetic |
| Ultrafast | No ("Fast" response times) | Not included | Not included | Included | Pro tiers article; pricing table for Plus |
| Live voice per rolling 24 hours | 3 hours, GPT-Live-1.1 | 15 hours | Unlimited, subject to abuse guardrails | Not listed | Voice article |
| Included usage against Plus | 1x | "5X standard usage allowance" for Codex, raised to "up to 10x" for a limited time in April | 10x for Work and Codex on a new subscription; grandfathered subscribers keep 20x through 29 October 2026, then 10x | 25x | Pro 100: OpenAI's 9 April 2026 release note. Pro 200 and Pro 500: [The New Stack, 29 September 2026](https://thenewstack.io/openai-halves-200-plan/), a news report; the Pro tiers article says only that new Pro 200 subscriptions get "a lower usage allowance" |

The Pro tiers article ranks the tiers without numbers: "Pro 200 includes more usage than Pro 100. Pro 500 offers the highest included usage of the three plans." Neither it nor the pricing page states a multiple of Plus as of 16 October 2026, and The New Stack's own report adds that "it's unclear what exactly the included allowance in the $500 plan will be".

One line of arithmetic per tier:

- Pro 100 costs $80 a month more than Plus, for a standard 5x of Plus's Codex allowance.
- Pro 200 costs $180 more, for a reported 10x of Plus on a new subscription.
- Pro 500 costs $480 more. Ultrafast draws the included allowance at 8 times the Standard rate, so a reported 25x spent entirely in Ultrafast covers about 3.1 Plus allowances of Standard-speed work (25 / 8).

The 9 April release note gives no end date for Pro 100's "limited time" 10x, and no later entry says it has ended. If it has, and the press figures hold, a new subscriber's usage rises in step with price: 5x, 10x and 25x of Plus for 5, 10 and 25 times the money. On those numbers Pro buys headroom, plus Ultrafast on Pro 500, rather than a lower price per unit of work.

One dated rule, from the Pro tiers article: if you had an active Pro 200 subscription at any point from 22 September through 10 a.m. Pacific Time on 29 September 2026, you keep the previous allowance through 29 October 2026, and every other Pro 200 subscription gets "a lower usage allowance" at the same $200.

### Rows the Pricing Table's Pro Column Adds Over Plus

Read on 16 October 2026, alongside the Pro column in the first table:

- GPT-5.6 Sol Pro, marked No on Free, Go and Plus, and on the Pro card "Pro reasoning powered by GPT-6 Astra"; the 7 October release note says the Pro reasoning option "continues to use GPT-6 Astra".
- GPT Instant context of 128K, about 250 pages of input, and GPT Reasoning context of 400K, about 680 pages, against 54K and 256K on Plus.
- Codex marked "Expanded". The Work and Codex pricing page says "Pro plans currently have no five-hour limit", while Plus works to OpenAI's estimates of 15 to 160 local messages per five hours on GPT-6.1 Sol and 5 to 45 on GPT-6 Astra, which it says "are not fixed message limits".
- Dots, OpenAI's "always-on agents", rolling out to eligible Pro users aged 18 and older; the 29 September 2026 release note excludes the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the UK at launch.
- Voice marked "Unlimited\*" (split by tier in the table above), GPT-6 Luna marked "Expanded" and "Share Plugins with your workspace" marked "Yes", against "Expanded", "Yes" and "No" on Plus.

For a marketer, the Pro trigger is a Codex or Work session that keeps stopping at the Plus five-hour estimates, or a source pack over about 320 pages. Drafting copy hits neither.

## ChatGPT Go vs Plus Price, Billing and Switching

As of 16 October 2026:

| | Go | Plus | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| US price | $8 a month | $20 a month | $100, $200 or $500 a month |
| Billing | Monthly | Monthly | Monthly |
| Annual plan (per OpenAI's help articles) | Not offered | Not offered | Not offered |

- Annual billing: the Go article says OpenAI does "not support annual billing or the option to pay for multiple months in advance for ChatGPT Go, Plus, or Pro subscriptions", and the pricing page's FAQ offers "annual plans for Business and Enterprise" only.
- Upgrading: OpenAI's 9 April 2026 release note says "You can upgrade, switch, or cancel your plan from Settings > My Plan or from the Pricing page."
- Downgrading to Go: no refund. The Go article says "Your current plan will remain active until the end of your billing cycle", after which the account moves to Go's rate.
- App store subscriptions: per the Pro tiers article, cancel "through the platform where you purchased your subscription: ChatGPT, Apple or Google Play".
- Credits past a limit: OpenAI's [credits article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12642688-using-credits-for-flexible-usage-in-chatgpt-personal-plans) says Plus and Pro users who hit a Codex limit see an option to add credits, while buying credits is open to "a limited group of Free and Go users".

## ChatGPT Go vs Plus: Pay for the Rows Your Week Hits

Go at $8 is enough when ChatGPT is where you write and GPT-6 Luna's drafts pass your edit. It has Plus's context windows, with response times OpenAI labels "Limited on bandwidth & availability" and possible ads.

Plus at $20 is worth it once your week needs chats on GPT-6 Sol, a screen with no ads, or one of the rows Go marks No or Limited and Plus marks Yes: deep research, the Office and Sheets extensions, Sites, image generation with Thinking or record mode. Pro makes sense only when Codex's five-hour estimates or an input of more than about 320 pages stop the work.

Re-read the pricing table and the Go, Plus and ads articles on the day you buy, then pay for the cheapest column that clears this week's jobs.
