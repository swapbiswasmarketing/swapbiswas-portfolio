**Five.** On 18 December 2025, Instagram's @creators account [posted on Threads](https://www.threads.com/@creators/post/DSalXGPCWM4): "New hashtag guidance: Starting today, Instagram will allow up to 5 hashtags in a reel or post." That is the Instagram hashtag limit as of November 2026, and I would write every caption to it and treat each 3 or 30 you still see as either an ended test or a different limit that has nothing to do with your caption.

The Threads page shows no date, so the date comes from [Social Media Today's report](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/) by Andrew Hutchinson, published 18 December 2025 and linking that post. The three numbers, side by side:

## What Is the Instagram Hashtag Limit in 2026?

**5 hashtags per reel or post.** Instagram's @creators account set that number on 18 December 2025, and no later Instagram announcement I found changes it as of 4 November 2026.

The Threads post ends "Swipe to learn more" over a set of images. Social Media Today quoted Instagram's fuller wording, linking that post:

> "Instagram will gradually update the number of hashtags that you can include in a caption for a reel or post to five. We find that using fewer (up to 5) more targeted hashtags, rather than many generic ones, can improve both your content's performance and people's experience on Instagram."

Three details in that wording decide how to read the rule:

- **Caption.** The limit is on what you put in a caption.
- **Reel or post.** Both formats are named. Stories and comments are not.
- **Gradually.** The post opens with "Starting today", while the fuller wording says Instagram "will gradually update" the number, so the cap did not have to reach every account on the same day.

Instagram's Help Center article "Use hashtags on Instagram" did not return its body text to a plain fetch on 4 November 2026: the page gave only its title and the line "You can add hashtags to your photos and videos on Instagram.", so I quote no number from it.

The 3-tag test was uneven while it ran: PiunikaWeb relays Reddit reports that the cap showed in the mobile app and not in a desktop browser. Those are user reports about a test that the 5 rule replaced, and no Instagram page I could read lists any exception to the 5.

## The 30 Hashtags Per Week Limit Is Meta's API

The "30 hashtags per week" figure is a quota on Meta's developer API. Meta's [Hashtag Search guide](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-facebook-login/hashtag-search), updated 17 August 2026, says:

- "You can query a maximum of 30 unique hashtags on behalf of an Instagram Business or Creator Account within a rolling, 7 day period."
- "Once you query a hashtag, it will count against this limit for 7 days."
- "Fetching media for a hashtag counts as querying it", including the recent media and top media requests.

The [IG Hashtag Search reference](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-hashtag-search) (updated 23 July 2024) states the same cap as "a maximum of 30 unique hashtags within a 7 day period."

That quota applies to software that looks up hashtags, such as a tool that pulls public posts tagged with a campaign hashtag onto a website wall. It does not touch how many hashtags you type. If a tool shows a weekly cap of 30 hashtags, that cap matches this API quota: it limits how many hashtags the tool can query for your account in 7 days.

A second Meta page still prints 30 for captions. The [IG User Media reference](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media), updated 28 September 2026, describes the caption field for posts published through the API as "Maximum 2200 characters, 30 hashtags, and 20 @ tags." I have not tested which number the API enforces today. If you schedule posts through a tool built on it, keep the caption to 5 anyway: that is the number Instagram announced for reels and posts. Meta's WhatsApp docs set their own caption cap, 1,024 characters for an image or video message sent through the API, listed with [the other WhatsApp character limits by field](/blog/whatsapp-character-limit/).

| Limit | Applies to | Number | Meta or Instagram page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hashtags in a reel or post caption | Anyone posting | 5 | @creators on Threads, 18 December 2025 |
| Hashtag lookups through the API | Apps acting for a Business or Creator account | 30 unique per rolling 7 days | Hashtag Search guide, 17 August 2026 |
| Caption field for API-published posts | Apps publishing for an account | 2,200 characters, 30 hashtags, 20 @ tags | IG User Media reference, 28 September 2026 |

## Does the Instagram Hashtag Limit Apply to Comments and Stories?

Instagram has not said. The @creators post covers "a reel or post", and the wording Social Media Today quotes names "a caption for a reel or post"; neither mentions comments or Stories.

Comments were an open question in February 2024 as well. Social Media Today's report on the five-tag pop-up, which Instagram later called a glitch, said "Maybe Instagram's also going to limit the discovery value of hashtags posted within comments", which was speculation, and Instagram's December 2025 wording did not settle it.

Until Instagram's Help Center or the @creators account states a rule for comments or Stories, I would plan as if:

- The caption carries your 5 hashtags, on reels and on feed posts.
- Moving extra tags into the first comment relies on a gap Instagram has neither confirmed nor closed.
- Stories sit outside what the announcement names, so check the app itself before relying on any number there.

## Do Hashtags Still Help Reach on Instagram?

Instagram ties the limit to relevance, not to a reach figure. Its quoted wording says fewer, more targeted hashtags "can improve both your content's performance and people's experience on Instagram", and Social Media Today adds that Instagram is "limiting this to five to avoid misuse."

Instagram chief Adam Mosseri has said the same about reach. Social Media Today's 18 December 2025 report, linking one of his reels, wrote that Mosseri "once again said that hashtags don't improve visibility on Instagram", while calling them "a great way to let people know what a post is about and connect posts."

Neither Instagram's quoted wording nor that report gives a number for reach, and neither the @creators post nor the wording Social Media Today quotes says that a post with more than 5 hashtags gets demoted. Any page that puts a percentage on the reach of a 30-tag post is giving its own figure, not Instagram's.

With five slots, the choice of tags matters more than the count. A rule I would use:

| Slot | What goes in it | Example for a B2B launch post |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The topic, as a searcher would type it | #productmarketing |
| 2 | The narrower topic inside it | #saaslaunch |
| 3 | The audience | #b2bmarketing |
| 4 | A campaign or event tag you own or join | Your launch or event hashtag |
| 5 | Optional: a brand tag, or leave it empty | #yourbrand |

The example tags are illustrations, not tags I have tracked. Skip broad tags such as #explore or #reels, the examples Business Today's report gives of the broad terms Instagram discourages. Other platforms draw the line elsewhere: YouTube's Help page ignores every hashtag on a video that carries more than 60, as set out in [the YouTube hashtag and tag limits](/blog/youtube-character-limit/).

The test I use for a platform limit is the one I applied to [LinkedIn's 220-character headline limit](/blog/linkedin-headline-character-limit/): the number counts when the platform's own page prints it. Instagram's 5 passes that test through the @creators post; I found no Instagram page behind the 3.

Other platforms count hashtags differently. On X, a hashtag counts toward the post's characters as the # plus the tag text, inside [X's weighted 280-character limit](/blog/x-character-limit/). When the same post goes to LinkedIn, the image and video specs from LinkedIn's Help pages are in [my LinkedIn post size reference](/blog/linkedin-post-size/).

## Write Every Caption to the Instagram Hashtag Limit of 5

The Instagram hashtag limit is 5 per reel or post, from Instagram's own @creators account, while the 3 belonged to a 2025 test and the 30 now lives mostly in Meta's developer docs. If you draft captions with AI, add "no more than 5 hashtags" to the constraints of any [social media prompt for ChatGPT](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-marketing/) you reuse for Instagram.

Instagram went from a 3-tag test to the 5 rule within a month in late 2025, so recheck the @creators account, Meta's two API pages and the Help Center each quarter, and watch for a stated rule on comments or Stories. Before your next post, cut the caption's hashtag list to the five tags that name its topic and audience.
