## Is Filmora Lifetime Really Lifetime?

It is lifetime for one version only. Wondershare's [store FAQ](https://filmora.wondershare.com/store/buy-filmora-video-editor.html) says: "If you buy a Perpetual License you will get lifetime access to the version of the software you purchased the license for, including updates. However, this plan does not include future versions of the software. Should a new version be released, you would need to purchase a new plan to access it."

The [shop page FAQ](https://filmora.wondershare.com/shop/buy/buy-video-editor.html) adds the definitions:

- An upgrade is "a less frequent, more drastic change", its example being "Filmora 15 to Filmora 16".
- "Perpetual plans include all updates to the current major version", and subscriptions include updates and upgrades during the subscription period.
- On the perpetual plan itself: "You may need to pay an additional lower-cost fee to resubscribe for support and upgrades."

So a Perpetual plan bought today covers Filmora 16 and its fixes. A Lifetime License from the Mac App Store or a "lifetime" listing elsewhere does not state its version terms on the listing, so I would price it as one version until the seller says otherwise.

## Is Filmora Free?

Yes, with a watermark. Wondershare's shop FAQ says the free edition gives "all of Filmora's basic editing features", but "watermarks will be added to the exported files", and Filmora Cloud Storage and the full Creative Assets library need a paid plan. The plan comparison on the same page gives the free edition 15 free AI Mate uses a day, against 500 a month on the paid plans.

If you need a free route for a short video, export one test clip from the free edition first and check whether the watermark matters for where it will be posted.
