Airtel customers who claimed the 12-month offer between July 2025 and **16 January 2026** got Perplexity Pro free, and for those with no paid plan the year ends 12 months after the claim, between about mid-July 2026 and mid-January 2027, going by [Perplexity's Airtel help page](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11842322-perplexity-pro-airtel-promo) and TelecomTalk's report of a 17 July 2025 claim start (linked below), so the earliest years have already ended. No other route on Perplexity's help pages gives Indian users Pro free today: everyone else gets the free Standard plan, or, as a verified student or educator, a discounted Education Pro plan priced in US dollars.

I read every route below on **31 October 2026**, logged out, from India; the one exception, the Pro price, is marked where it appears. In my view the more useful question in India now is what happens when an Airtel free year ends, and what it renews into.

No, not for new users. Airtel's 12 free months closed to new claims on 16 January 2026, students get a discount rather than free Pro, and the referral programs have ended. Airtel claimants have Pro for 12 months from their start, so early claimants' years have already ended.

## Has the Airtel Perplexity Pro Offer Ended?

Yes. Perplexity's Airtel article, last modified on 2 October 2026, says "The promotional offer ended on January 16, 2026." What the offer was, in that article's words:

- "twelve (12) months of complimentary access to Perplexity Pro" for "Airtel customers in India", including business and enterprise customers.
- Open to new, current and former Perplexity subscribers.
- Redeemed only through the Airtel Thanks app, under "Claim OTTs and more" in the Benefits section.
- "You must provide a credit card to redeem the offer. You will not be charged during the promotional period."
- "The promotion is for Perplexity Pro only, and not valid for Enterprise Pro or Max subscriptions."

Neither landing page carries the offer now. On 31 October 2026, Perplexity's `perplexity.ai/airtel` returned a 404, and Airtel's `airtel.in/perplexity` returned a 200 page whose served HTML had no Perplexity text in it. [TelecomTalk reported on 18 February 2026](https://telecomtalk.info/airtels-perplexity-pro-ends-existing-12month-access/1004521/) that the claim window ran from July 17, 2025 until January 17, 2026, one day later than Perplexity's date, and that people who activated before the deadline keep the full 12 months. Go by Perplexity's date, since its page is the one that governs your account.

## Already Claimed Airtel's Offer? What Renews and How to Cancel

The renewal rule sits in Perplexity's [billing FAQ for Pro subscribers](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10353002-billing-faq-for-pro-plan-subscribers), last modified 20 October 2026, under trial and promotional subscriptions, a section that names partner promotions as an example:

- "A valid credit or debit card is required for all promotional and trial redemptions."
- "No charges are applied during your promotional/trial period."
- "Unless you cancel before the trial ends, your subscription will auto-renew at the standard rate for the plan you selected during redemption (monthly or annual)."
- If Perplexity asks an existing promotional user for a card, you have 7 days to add one or the account drops to the free version, and missed days are not added back.

If you claimed early, open Manage Subscription on perplexity.ai to see whether a renewal charge has already gone through; if it has, the refund windows at the end of this section apply.

Cancel on the platform where the subscription lives. Perplexity's [cancellation article](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10354283-canceling-a-subscription) gives these paths:

| Where it is billed | Cancel path, as Perplexity words it |
|---|---|
| perplexity.ai (web) | Settings, then "Subscription" under Perplexity Pro, "Manage Subscription", then "Cancel subscription" |
| iPhone (App Store) | App Store, profile icon, "Subscriptions", "Perplexity - Ask Anything", "Cancel subscription" |
| Android (Google Play) | Google Play, "Payments & Subscriptions", "Perplexity - Ask Anything", "Cancel subscription" |

What happens next, from the same pages:

- Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the current period; the account then reverts "to the free version".
- Deleting your account does not cancel the subscription, so cancel first.
- Never update Perplexity payment details through the App Store or Google Play. The billing FAQ warns this "will create a new, separate subscription and you will be charged."
- If a renewal charge has already gone through, India is not one of the regions with longer windows on the [refunds page](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10354288-refunds), so the "All other customers" rule applies: a refund request within 24 hours of purchase for monthly plans, and a full refund within 72 hours for annual plans.

One more check if Pro has gone missing: the free months are tied to the account you used at activation. Perplexity's [partner promotions page](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11385821-partner-promotions-and-referral-programs) says to search your inbox for the "Welcome to Pro" email to find which account that was, and to treat your Perplexity account status, rather than the Airtel app, as the source of truth.

## Is Perplexity Pro Free for Students in India?

No. Perplexity's current student offer is a discount: [Education Pro](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/12590157-what-is-education-pro) "is offered to verified students and educators at a discount", and the plan comparison page lists it at **$10 a month** with SheerID verification. The [Comet students page](https://www.perplexity.ai/grow/comet/students) headline read "Get Comet with discounted Perplexity Pro. The AI browser built for students." on 31 October 2026.

In February 2025, [YourStory reported](https://yourstory.com/2025/02/perplexity-ai-free-access-indian-students) that "Perplexity AI offers free pro version to Indian students". The help pages read today describe a paid, discounted plan instead.

How the route works, per Perplexity's [student verification article](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/12162294-how-to-verify-your-student-status) and the Education Pro page:

1. Create your Perplexity account with any email, personal or student.
2. In an existing account, click upgrade in the bottom left corner, toggle to the Education plan, and click "Verify as student".
3. SheerID checks your enrolment. If your institution is missing from SheerID's list, you can ask SheerID to add it.
4. If you are verified but checkout fails, open perplexity.ai in a desktop browser, click Upgrade Plan, stay in the Personal view and select Pro; Perplexity says the discount applies at checkout.
5. The discount does not move with you: "student discounts are not transferrable", so a new email means verifying again.

If a free student plan matters more than the brand, Google's [Gemini student offer](/blog/gemini-student-offer/) gives eligible students in India a free year if they redeem by 31 December 2026, and my breakdown of [Claude's routes for Indian students](/blog/claude-student-discount-india/) covers Anthropic's options.

Perplexity's website shows no rupee price for India, so I have not converted the dollar price. Apple's App Store India listing, read on 31 October 2026, lists Perplexity Pro in-app purchases at ₹1,999 and ₹19,900 without naming the billing period; those are Apple's charges, not the web price. On the web, your bank's exchange rate and any foreign-transaction charge on your card decide the rupee amount, and prices change, so check the Pro page at checkout.
