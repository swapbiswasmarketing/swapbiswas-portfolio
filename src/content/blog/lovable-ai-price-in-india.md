Among the vendor and store pages I checked on 10 October 2026, rupee prices for Lovable appeared in one place: [Lovable's page on Apple's App Store in India](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/lovable-build-apps-with-ai/id6757471107), which sells a Pro Plan for **₹3,999** and a Business Plan for **₹7,900** inside the iPhone app. Any rupee figure you see for the website on a public page is a conversion, and the Lovable AI price in India starts from Lovable's dollar list.

The iPhone app sells one non-renewing Pro month for ₹3,999. The Free plan grants up to 30 build credits a month.

The web column comes from Lovable's [subscription plans docs](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/subscription-plans), which label the plan tiers "Prices in USD", and its [credits and usage docs](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/credits-and-usage) for the top-ups. Prices and limits like these change: each row is as read on 10 October 2026, and the links above let you check any of them again.

My advice from these lists: price Lovable from India on the web, in dollars. The app's Pro Plan at ₹3,999, with tax included, is about **66%** above the ₹2,415 web conversion, which leaves out any tax the web checkout adds. The gap shows up in the US too, where the app's Pro Plan costs $39.99, **60% more** than the $25 web price for Pro.

## Lovable Pro Price in India on the iPhone App

On Apple's India App Store, Lovable's Pro Plan is ₹3,999 and its Business Plan ₹7,900. [Lovable's mobile app docs](https://docs.lovable.dev/integrations/lovable-mobile-app) set the rules for these purchases:

- "workspaces on the Free plan can buy a plan, and workspaces on Pro and Business plans can buy credit top-ups, even when the plan itself was bought on the web."
- "Plans purchased in the iOS app cover one month at a time and do not renew automatically." When the month ends the workspace moves to the Free plan, and until then the plan card shows "Managed by App Store" ([manage subscription docs](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/manage-subscription)).
- Upgrading or changing a plan is "Not available in the app", and Apple handles receipts and refunds.
- The app shares "the same account, projects, and credits as the web app".

Every in-app item on the two listings, read on 10 October 2026:

- The India prices sit at about ₹100 per App Store dollar, 2.2% to 3.5% above RBI's 9 October rate. Apple's rule is that "Prices advertised to customers in all countries except the U.S. and Canada include taxes" ([Apple's 2023 tech talk on App Store pricing](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/110364/)), so the India figures include tax and the US ones do not.
- The Pro Plan is priced the same as 100 Credits (Mobile Pro) on both listings, but neither listing nor Lovable's docs state how many credits the plan includes.
- The 25-credit packs have no web equivalent, since the web's smallest top-up is 50 credits.
- Inside the iPhone app Apple takes the payment, and [Apple's payment methods page for India](https://support.apple.com/en-in/111741) lists Apple Account balance, Mastercard, Net Banking for adding funds only, UPI and Visa.

Android works differently. Lovable's docs say "On Android, in-app purchases aren't available yet", so an Android user checks out on lovable.dev with Stripe, and [Google Play's India listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=dev.lovable.build&hl=en_IN&gl=IN) showed no Lovable price on 10 October 2026.

## Is Lovable Free?

Yes. Lovable's Free plan costs $0 and grants 5 build credits a day up to 30 a month, 20 Cloud credits and 4 AI credits a month, and a daily chat allowance, per the plan docs.

| On the Free plan | What Lovable's pages say |
|---|---|
| Daily build credits | Reset every day at 00:00 UTC and do not roll over |
| Daily chat allowance | For messages in Chats and Chat mode; resets every day at 00:00 UTC, no rollover. No size is given, and the [credits and usage docs' chat costs section](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/credits-and-usage#chat-costs) says this chat pricing, allowance included, is temporary and applies through 31 October 2026 |
| AI credits | The 4 a month are for AI features in the apps you build |
| Domains | 5 lovable.app domains, per the pricing page; no custom domains |
| Code | No code editing and no code download |
| Badge and support | The "Edit with Lovable" badge stays; community support only |

The 30-credit cap is reached early in the month. The plan docs give this example: on Free, "you receive daily build credits for the first 6 days each month" (6 x 5 = 30), "then none until the next month starts", and grants resume on the 1st at 00:00 UTC. Standard Pro and Business plans have no monthly cap on the daily credits, per the plan docs, though the pricing page FAQ says "some regional plans cap these at 30 per month".

## Is Lovable Free for Students in India?

No, but students and teachers can get 50% off Pro for up to 12 months, which [Lovable's students page](https://lovable.dev/students) puts at "$12.50/month", about ₹1,208 at the reference rate before any tax. The page and the [plan docs' discount section](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/subscription-plans#student-and-teacher-discount) word the offer differently:

| Question | Plan docs | Students page |
|---|---|---|
| Which plans? | "It applies only to the Pro plan with 100 credits per month, billed monthly. Higher Pro credit tiers, annual billing, and other plans are not available with the discount." | "Students receive 50% off on the Lovable Pro 100 credits monthly plan, or a discount of $12.50/month on all other plans." |
| How is status checked? | The domain of your account's email address, "against a list of academic institutions" | "your educational email or student ID" |
| Who can apply? | "Verification is available only to workspaces on the Free plan" | "This discount is only valid for users on the free plan." |
| How long? | "up to 12 months" | "valid for up to 12 months" |
| After you cancel | "Lovable does not offer the discount again after you cancel." | "you will lose your discount and can not reactivate it later" |
| Teachers | "The plan name also covers verified teachers." | "If you are teaching at a university or school you can still get a student discount." |

For a student in India, what the pages add up to:

- Neither page limits the discount by country, and the students page says Lovable accepts students "from most accredited universities and colleges worldwide". Whether your institution's email domain is on Lovable's list shows at verification, and the docs describe a Request review button for a domain it does not recognise.
- Plan on Pro 100 billed monthly, the one plan both pages agree on.
- The discount is a web checkout. Apple's India listing has no student item.
- Nonprofits get a separate deal: "a 20% discount to verified nonprofit organizations for one year", applied for by email with proof of status, per the same page's FAQ.

Claude, by comparison, has no student price on an individual plan, and [the Claude routes open to a student in India](/blog/claude-student-discount-india/) are sorted by who has to act on each.
