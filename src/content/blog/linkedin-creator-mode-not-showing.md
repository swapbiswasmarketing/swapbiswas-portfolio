The creator mode toggle that guides still send you to, labelled "Creator mode: Off" in the Resources section of your LinkedIn profile, is the one LinkedIn's Help notice [Updates to Creator Mode](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a5999182) scheduled for removal in **March 2024**. That is why LinkedIn creator mode not showing is the expected result: the features it bundled now sit in separate settings and access rules, or are gone. LinkedIn's notice is itself out of date on two rows, so the current answer for each feature is the Help page LinkedIn keeps for it, with its Last updated stamp.

Three of the eleven creator mode guides I checked on 10 October 2026 still give the steps for turning that toggle on, the newest of them a forum answer dated 29 July 2025. A fourth guide's address now redirects to an article that never mentions creator mode. The other seven say creator mode is gone, yet four still list Audio Events as something you can use and none names the plans the profile link now needs.

That is how LinkedIn's own notice still reads. It calls the profile link available through a custom button that a later Help page limits to a select group of paid subscribers, and it says creator mode members keep Audio Events, whose native creation LinkedIn ended in December 2024.

## Why Is LinkedIn Creator Mode Not Showing?

LinkedIn creator mode is not showing because LinkedIn removed it: its Updates to Creator Mode notice set February 2024 for removing profile hashtags and March 2024 for the creator mode toggle. The Follow or Connect choice sits in Settings & Privacy, under Visibility, Followers, [Make follow primary](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a528011), and changes can take up to 24 hours.

## Two Rows Where LinkedIn's 2024 Notice Is Out of Date

### The Profile Link Became a Premium Custom Button

Creator mode let you "add a link to the introduction section of your profile". The notice marks that row Available and points to the custom button, and three LinkedIn pages now describe the link three ways:

| LinkedIn page | Last updated, read 10 Oct 2026 | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| Updates to Creator Mode | 2 years ago | "You can add a link to your profile by using the feature Add or remove a custom button from your profile." Status: Available |
| [Adding website link to profile available through Premium Business plan](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a727760) | 1 year ago | "We have removed the option to add a hyperlink to your profile, which was previously supported through Creator Mode and is no longer available today." The custom button "is available to Premium Business subscribers, and Sales Navigator and Recruiter customers." |
| Add or remove a custom button from your profile | 9 months ago | "This feature is available only to a select group of LinkedIn Premium Business, Sales Navigator, and Recruiter Lite subscribers. This feature may be gradually available to more LinkedIn users in the future." |

The two later pages word eligibility differently: one names Recruiter customers, the other Recruiter Lite subscribers, and only the newer one says "a select group". I would plan around the narrower, newer wording, which means a paid subscription may still not show you the button. If you are weighing one anyway, I list [what Premium Business, Sales Navigator and Recruiter Lite are reported to cost in rupees](/blog/linkedin-premium-cost-in-india/), since LinkedIn's own plan pages show no rupee price for any of them to a visitor in India who is not signed in. Where your link can go, by account:

| Your account | Where the link goes | LinkedIn's terms |
|---|---|---|
| Premium Business, Sales Navigator or Recruiter Lite, inside the select group | A custom button labelled Visit my store, Visit my website, View my portfolio, View my blog, Book an appointment, View my newsletter or View my services, shown on your profile, in search results, in messages and in the feed | If you list services, adding it "will replace the services on your profile" |
| Any other account | Up to three websites in the Contact info section of your introduction, with no button | "display up to three website links to your profile", with no plan named on LinkedIn's [website help page](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a548010) |

### Native Audio Events Creation Ended on 2 December 2024

The notice tells members who had LinkedIn Live, Audio Events and Newsletter with creator mode on that they "will still have access to these sharing tools". It is still written in the future tense about February and March 2024. LinkedIn's Audio Events page, linked in the table above, reads differently:

- "Since December 2, 2024, the creation of native Audio Events is no longer available." An Audio Event scheduled to take place before 31 December 2024 could still be hosted "as usual"; one scheduled after that date had to be recreated as a LinkedIn Live event.
- "With LinkedIn Live, you can no longer host an audio-only event natively on LinkedIn." An audio-only event now runs as a LinkedIn Live event, broadcast from a third-party streaming tool.

LinkedIn's [Sharing tools access criteria](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a529748) page, last updated 1 year ago, has rows for LinkedIn Live, the Follow link and Newsletter, and none for Audio Events.

## How to Get a Follow Button Instead of Connect on LinkedIn

Make follow primary is the setting that carries creator mode's Follow button today. On desktop, LinkedIn's follower settings page gives these steps:

1. Click the Me icon at the top of your LinkedIn homepage.
2. Select Settings & Privacy from the dropdown.
3. Select Visibility on the left pane.
4. Under Visibility of your LinkedIn activity, click Followers.
5. Under Make follow primary, turn the toggle on to show Follow, or off to show Connect.

On mobile, tap your profile picture, tap Settings & Privacy, tap the Visibility tab, then tap Followers in the Visibility of your LinkedIn activity section. LinkedIn notes that "Changes can take up to 24 hours to take effect."

The same screen holds Who can follow you. Choose Everyone on LinkedIn if you want followers beyond your connections: LinkedIn warns that if you limit followers to your connections, "followers outside your network will be removed and your follower number will be the same as your total connections." The choice comes up on layoff and job-search posts, where strangers turn up in the comments; [how to write a layoff post commenters can act on](/blog/cfbr-meaning-linkedin/) covers what to ask them for in place of CFBR.

### Reasons a Profile Shows Follow Instead of Connect

| Reason | Where LinkedIn says it |
|---|---|
| Creator mode was on before the change | The notice's Follow sentence, quoted in the feature table |
| The owner turned on the Make follow primary toggle | Manage who can follow you settings, steps above |
| Over 500 connections | [Missing connect button on profile](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a548094), 2 years ago: "You have over 500 connections. By default, the Connect button will be replaced with Follow" |

The Missing connect button page mixes the visitor ("You're already connected to that member") and the profile owner ("You've selected Follow as the default option") in one list, so it does not say whose 500 connections count. And since the settings page presents Make follow primary as a toggle you turn on, I read the notice's sentence as a carry-over for members who had creator mode on, not a default for every profile; one guide in the check below reads it the other way.

With Follow as your primary button:

- Visitors who know you can still connect: LinkedIn's [help on connecting through a Follow button](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a523224) says to click More in your introduction section and select Connect, and that for some members "you may be prompted to enter the member's email address". If you decline, the sender keeps following you until they unfollow, per the Follow and connect page.
- The Follow link sits under Sharing on socials in your Contact info, as a link or as a code snippet for a button.

### Follow or Connect as Your Primary Button

A decision rule built on how LinkedIn's Follow and connect on LinkedIn page describes the two actions:

| If the people who land on your profile | Set | LinkedIn's basis |
|---|---|---|
| Mostly know you from your posts, and you publish on a schedule | Follow | "Following lets you learn from someone's content, while connecting builds a two-way relationship." |
| Are people you work with, hire or sell to | Connect | Connections can "message each other directly and see each other's posts and updates in your feeds" |

For sales, Taplio, one of the guides in the check below, suggests Connect plus a custom "Book a call" button. That needs the custom button, which the profile link section above shows is limited to some paid subscribers; without it, put the booking link in Contact info.

If your posts are the distribution side of a [thought leadership program](/blog/thought-leadership-content/), or you want a Follow link for your site or email signature, the Follow row describes you. Before you switch, write down your profile analytics with the date, and take the after reading only once the window you are comparing has turned over, the same before-and-after routine as [reading LinkedIn impressions on your experience after a profile edit](/blog/linkedin-impressions-on-your-experience/). The 24 hours LinkedIn mentions is only how long the button can take to change.

## LinkedIn Creator Mode Not Showing? Check Each Feature's Own Page

Any fix for LinkedIn creator mode not showing that sends you to the Resources section describes LinkedIn before March 2024. A guide built on the 2024 notice inherits its two stale rows unless it checks the feature pages, and LinkedIn's own [Help search for creator mode](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/search?q=creator%20mode) still puts that notice at the top of its results.

Nine of the fourteen rows in the feature table have a current Help page with its own Last updated stamp. Before you plan around a creator mode feature, open its page and read the stamp.

If your posts are what bring people to your profile, open Settings & Privacy and set your primary button to Follow.
