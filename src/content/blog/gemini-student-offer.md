"Unless cancelled earlier, Google AI Plus will automatically charge ₹399/month after the trial ends," reads the fine print at the foot of [Google's India student page](https://gemini.google/in/students/?hl=en-IN) (read logged out from India, 31 October 2026). The Gemini student offer above that line is one year of Google AI Plus at no cost for university students aged 18 and over who verify through SheerID, and it must be redeemed by **31 December 2026**.

The same page carries a second line worth reading before you claim: "Eligibility must be verified each year to maintain access." Both lines, and the redeem-by date, come from that page, which I read after Cloudflare's trace returned `loc=IN`; the account and age rules are in [Google's offer terms](https://one.google.com/offer/studentoffer8?g1_landing_page=0&hl=en-IN), last updated 19 August 2026.

Treat this as a 12-month AI Plus trial with a rupee bill attached, rather than the free AI Pro year that July 2025 headlines still describe, and decide on the day you claim whether you will cancel before month 13.

## What Is the Gemini Student Offer?

> The Gemini student offer in India is a one-year Google AI Plus plan at no cost: 400 GB of storage, 2x usage limits and Gemini Omni. It is open to university students aged 18+ who verify through SheerID on a personal Google Account. Redeem by 31 December 2026; after 12 months it charges ₹399 a month unless you cancel.

Google's rules for it, read on 31 October 2026:

| Rule | Google's wording | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Plan | "Claim your one-year Google AI Plus student plan today at no cost" | India student page |
| What is included | 2x "higher usage limits, 400 GB of cloud storage, access to Gemini Omni, plus unlimited uploads for Study Notebooks, Visuals and Live" | India student page |
| Length | "the promoted 12-month period from the day that you redeem the Offer" | Offer terms |
| Last date | "Offer expires and must be redeemed by 31 December 2026" | Offer terms |
| Age | "Be 18 years of age or older" | Offer terms |
| Study | "a student enrolled at a higher education institution" | Offer terms |
| Proof | "Verify your student status through SheerID using a valid school email address" | [Google One Help](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/17422238?hl=en-IN) |
| Account | "Have a personal Google Account"; the offer "is not available on your school-issued Workspace for Education account" | Offer terms |

| Usage limits | "you may experience different usage limits than Google One members on a paid plan. Limits may change without notice" | Offer terms |

Add-ons are excluded too: Google One Help says upgraded storage ("2 TB for Google AI Plus") and recurring add-ons "are unavailable with a Google One student offer". Prices, limits and plan contents change, so check the student page before you claim.

## How to Claim Google's Student Plan

Google One Help gives these steps, and the "Claim a student plan at no cost" button on the India student page links to the same address:

1. Go to one.google.com/ai-student. Logged out, it redirected me to a Google sign-in page on 31 October 2026.
2. Sign in to the personal Google Account you want the plan on, and stay on that account until the end.
3. Select the student offer.
4. Verify your student status through SheerID when asked.
5. Complete the subscription flow, adding a form of payment if the account has none.

[SheerID's student FAQ](https://verify.sheerid.com/student-faq-b/) lists what it asks for: your full school name, your "First and last name, as shown on school records", your date of birth and a "School or personal email address". If it asks for proof, it wants a school-issued document showing your name, school name and a "Date showing current enrollment", such as a "School ID card with expiration date", a "Class schedule" or a "Tuition receipt".

## Gemini Student Offer Not Working or Not Eligible: Google's Causes

Every cause below is one Google or SheerID states. The India student page sends trouble to Google One Help, and that article names one error by its cause: "you may be signed in with a different Google Account to the one that you were using before you verified your student status." The fix it gives is to sign in and claim "with the Google Account that you were using when you first selected a student offer and got sent to SheerID."

The other blocks, checked on 31 October 2026:

- [ ] Age under 18. The terms require "18 years of age or older", and Google's [India plan page](https://gemini.google/in/subscriptions/?hl=en-IN) says Google AI plans "are only available for ages 18+".
- [ ] School-issued Workspace for Education account. The terms say the offer is not available on it; use a personal Google Account.
- [ ] Family group membership. Google One Help excludes "Family group members, unless they leave their shared plan first."
- [ ] Google One through a third party. The terms exclude "a discounted Pixel bundle subscriber" and anyone with "a Google One subscription through a third party or affiliate such as Google Fi"; Google One Help adds "Pixel Pass".
- [ ] Supervised account. "You also do not qualify if you have a supervised Google Account."
- [ ] An earlier upgrade out of the trial. "If you upgrade to a paid membership from the 12-month trial, you will not be eligible for the trial offer again."
- [ ] No qualifying payment method. The terms ask for "a Google Payments account with a qualifying form of payment at sign-up".
- [ ] No SheerID option that fits. Its FAQ says "If on the form you don't see an option that is a close fit, then that means you unfortunately are not eligible for this offer."
- [ ] A failed purchase after SheerID verifies you. SheerID says it "only handles the verification process" and points you to "the brand's customer support".
- [ ] An institution in an excluded country. Google One Help requires a student "at a higher education institution in a country or region where Google AI Plus is available (excluding Albania, Bolivia, Canada, Hong Kong, Macau, Tunisia and the United States)."

Before you claim, two switching rules can cost you something you already have:

| You are on | What Google One Help says happens |
|---|---|
| Another Google One extended trial | "Signing up for a student offer while on another Google One extended trial revokes your current offer and switches you to the new one." |
| A paid Google AI Pro plan | "you'll remain a Google AI Pro member until the end of your current billing cycle" |

The terms end on a catch-all: "Google reserves the right to determine eligibility at its sole discretion."

## After 12 Months: ₹399 a Month, Cancelling and Storage

When the Offer Period ends, the terms say your payment method "will be automatically charged the standard monthly subscription price for the applicable plan included in your Offer for your country, until you cancel your subscription", except where the law requires consent. For India the student page puts that at **₹399 a month**, and Google One Help says "You will be notified ahead of the upcoming change." To avoid the charge, the terms say "you must cancel before the end of the Offer Period."

[Google One Help's cancel article](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9056360?hl=en-IN) gives these steps on a computer:

1. Go to Google One.
2. Click Settings, then Cancel membership.
3. Click Cancel membership again to confirm.

On an Android phone, the same article says to open the Google One app, tap Menu, then Settings, then Cancel membership.

What cancelling does:

| Question | Google's wording |
|---|---|
| Do I lose the rest of the free year? | "your membership may remain active until the end of the Offer Period" (offer terms) |
| What storage do I keep? | "Each person will keep their default storage of up to 15 GB at no charge." (cancel article) |
| What if I am over 15 GB? | You "will not be able to store anything new" until you delete files or buy a membership (offer terms); in Gmail, "You can't send or receive messages" (cancel article) |

On re-verification, Google's pages differ. The India student page says "Eligibility must be verified each year to maintain access", and the terms make eligibility a condition "to retain access for the whole Offer Period". Google One Help's question "Do I need to reverify with SheerID?" answers yes "if you have claimed a Google AI Pro student offer", "usually every 12 months, for up to four consecutive years", and does not name the AI Plus trial. The offer terms contain no re-verification date. Keep your student email working for the whole year.
