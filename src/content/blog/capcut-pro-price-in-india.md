So the CapCut Pro price in India has no official answer, and I would treat any rupee figure for it as somebody's conversion or a reseller's price rather than CapCut's.

- **Store listings on 4 November 2026:** the India App Store and Google Play India pages for CapCut returned not found; the US pages for the same app were live.
- **US price, for reference only:** capcut.com showed Pro at **USD 19.99 a month** or USD 179.99 a year. That is the dollar price the page displayed, not an India price.

## Is CapCut Banned in India?

Yes, as far as the dated record shows. Inc42 reported in August 2020 that CapCut was among the apps blocked in July 2020, the news feed below carried no report of an unban, and CapCut was still missing from India's app stores on 4 November 2026. The record of when it was blocked is thinner than some of the pages quoted further down suggest:
