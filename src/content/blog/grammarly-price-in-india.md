- The In-App Purchases list carries ten entries with only those three prices: Annual Plan five times, Monthly Plan three times and Quarterly Plan twice. Apple does not say why names repeat, so the price on the confirmation screen is the one to trust.
- The item names do not say which plan they buy. The listing's description does: "Further improve your writing with Grammarly Premium".

## Grammarly Premium vs Pro vs Plus: Three Names, Three Checkouts

Grammarly sells the paid plan under three names, and each name belongs to a checkout. Its [Learn about Grammarly Pro article](https://support.grammarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/25043801131149-Learn-about-Grammarly-Pro) says "Grammarly Premium is available through the App Store and Google Play", while Pro is "Available via web purchase". Plus is "a region-specific plan available in select countries", per the About Grammarly Plus article.

| Plan | Where Grammarly sells it | AI prompts a month | Team seats | Takes the SheerID offer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | Website and apps | 100 | Not applicable | Not applicable |
| Premium | App Store and Google Play | 1,000 | Individual, no team features or admin controls | No |
| Pro | Website | 2,000 per member | Up to 149 | Yes |
| Plus | Website, in "select countries" | 2,000 | Up to 149 | No |
| Enterprise | Contact sales, "available through Superhuman Go" | Unlimited per member | "For larger organizations" | Not applicable |

The Free, Pro and Enterprise rows come from Grammarly's plans page, Premium's from the Learn about Grammarly Pro article, and Plus's from the About Grammarly Plus article. What the renames mean for a buyer:

- Pro replaced Premium and Business on the web. Grammarly's [trial article](https://support.grammarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033548272-Does-Grammarly-offer-a-trial) says "Grammarly Pro replaces Grammarly Premium and Grammarly Business plans", and the Learn about Grammarly Pro article says Premium bought on Grammarly's website is upgraded to Pro "at no extra cost", gradually.
- App Store and Google Play Premium stays as it is: "your Premium plan isn't changing." Pro "is currently not available" to Premium customers who bought through either store.
- Plus is closed to anyone on another paid plan, "such as Grammarly Premium, Pro, Business, or Grammarly for Education." To switch, Grammarly says to cancel, wait for the plan to end, then buy Plus at grammarly.com/upgrade/plus "if your billing country and payment details meet eligibility requirements."
- The company behind the plans has changed too. Several of Grammarly's help articles carry the note "Grammarly is now part of Superhuman", which is also the developer name on both app listings.

- The [About Grammarly Plus article](https://support.grammarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/43053519199885-About-Grammarly-Plus) gives no rupee figure. It says "the cost may vary by region" and that "Grammarly Plus is offered at a lower regional price than Grammarly Pro at a discounted global price."
- Eligibility depends on your billing details: Plus "is available to customers whose payment method and billing country match one of the regions where Grammarly Plus is offered." If you do not qualify, "Grammarly Pro also remains available at a fixed global price".

On tax, Grammarly's [tax article](https://support.grammarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/33154429182861-Is-tax-included-in-the-subscription-cost) says it "may charge applicable taxes based on your billing address", shown either "as a separate fee or included in the subscription cost". The India page says nothing about GST.

Grammarly has a help article for one renewal problem with Indian cards. Its [article on Indian credit cards](https://support.grammarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/4412769756557-I-have-an-Indian-credit-card-on-file-and-my-renewal-payment-doesn-t-go-through) says a failed renewal is "most likely" due to a Reserve Bank of India regulation "that all renewal payments need to be approved by the customer", and the fix is to re-enter the card under Payment Method on the Subscription page, or to use another card or PayPal.

## Grammarly Student Discount in India: The SheerID Rules

Grammarly's [SheerID offer article](https://support.grammarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/39623389691021-Grammarly-promo-offers-on-SheerID) says eligible users can save up to 50% on Grammarly Pro through SheerID. It states the saving in US dollars and gives no rupee figure. The rules, in Grammarly's words:

- **Who:** nine groups, from "Grammarly for Students" and "Grammarly for Graduate Students" to K-12 and university educators, young adults, military, first responders, healthcare workers and seniors.
- **Proof:** SheerID "may ask you to provide an official document issued by your organization or institution", and "This process can take up to seven business days." Approval arrives as a promo link from verify@sheerid.com.
- **How long:** "valid for one subscription period only. If you upgrade at a promotional rate, your subscription will be set to renew at a standard rate."
- **Seats:** "Promo offers apply to the first seat only."
- **Stacking:** offers "cannot be combined with other offers, and cannot be applied to previous purchases or purchases made via the App Store or Google Play Store."

Neither the SheerID offer article nor Grammarly's [article for schools missing from the list](https://support.grammarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/32619189344653-I-can-t-request-a-discount-on-SheerID-because-my-organization-or-school-is-not-on-the-list) names any country, so they do not settle whether a particular Indian college is accepted. The school field on the application form is where that shows. If your school does not appear, the second article says to keep typing its full name without abbreviations, or ask SheerID to add it; "SheerID will review your request within 30 days". A "Verification Limit Exceeded" error means "an application in your name has already been received this year."

The order matters for an iPhone buyer. A student who subscribes in the app at ₹4,699 cannot apply the offer to that purchase afterwards, and Grammarly's Pro article says Pro "is currently not available" to Premium customers who bought through the App Store or Google Play.

If your college has a Grammarly for Education licence, Grammarly says students join it through the school's identity provider, such as Okta, Microsoft Entra ID or Google Workspace, or by signing up with a school email at grammarly.com/enterprise/signup. Adobe takes a different route in India, printing a rupee student price on its own India site, which [my Adobe student plan breakdown](/blog/adobe-student-plan-india/) sets against the renewal rate.

## Does a Grammarly Promo Code Work?

No, not a code you found online. Grammarly's [discounts article](https://support.grammarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038523451-Does-Grammarly-offer-discounts) says "Grammarly does not provide discounts in the form of coupons or other codes. If you found a promo code online, it is not a promotional offer from Grammarly."

Grammarly names two discount channels:

- Occasional promotional offers to some email subscribers, which you opt into through special-offer emails on your Profile page.
- The SheerID offers above.

Both carry the same rules: one subscription period, first seat only, no combining, and no App Store or Google Play purchases.
