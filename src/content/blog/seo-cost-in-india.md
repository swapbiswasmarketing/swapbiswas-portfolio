Expert Web World's Gold plan, BeTopSEO's Growth plan and the bottom of Digital Braino's Starter band each list about ₹15,000 a month on the pages I read on 16 October 2026, and each names keywords, on-page work and something different on top: 200 manual directory submissions at Expert Web World (up to 20 keywords, plus 18% GST); 5 blog posts and 50 backlinks at BeTopSEO (5 to 10 keywords, ₹14,999); and a one-time site audit and Google Business Profile setup at Digital Braino. Across the 10 agencies in my check that printed rupee prices for their own plans, SEO cost in India came to a median of **₹16,250 a month** for the cheapest plan and **₹47,500** for the dearest.

Because the same list price can stand for such different work, I think SEO packages should be compared by their line items first and their price second, and checked against [Google's Do you need an SEO? page](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/do-i-need-seo). This page covers monthly retainers; for a one-off audit, [my breakdown of what an SEO audit costs](/blog/how-much-does-an-seo-audit-cost/) has the numbers.

SEO charges in India started at ₹5,000 to ₹70,000 a month on the 10 agency pages in my check with rupee prices on 16 October 2026, with medians of ₹16,250 for the cheapest plan and ₹47,500 for the dearest. Only 3 of them say how tax applies, and 12 of the 24 agencies publish no price list of their own.

## What Does a Rs 5,000 SEO Package Include?

The one ₹5,000 plan in the check, Expert Web World's Bronze, includes up to 5 keywords, title and meta tag work on 7 pages plus 11 other on-page and technical items, and 80 directory, bookmarking, classified and article submissions. The ₹5,000 is an offer price, and with 18% GST the bill comes to ₹5,900 a month. Every row where the Bronze column shows a number or YES, grouped:

- up to 5 keywords, with a recommended SEO time period of 6 months
- title tag and meta tag optimisation on 7 web pages, and competitor analysis for up to 2 competitors
- YES on 11 more on-page and technical rows: keyword management, H1/H2/H3 tags, anchor tags, image tags, SEO-friendly URLs, content optimisation, W3C validation, sitemap, robots.txt, GA4 setup and website speed analysis
- 50 manual directory submissions, 10 social bookmarking submissions (the row names Scoop.it and Diigo), 10 manual classified ad postings and 10 article submissions
- a Google local listing, and video optimisation marked "1 PM"
- Facebook fan page creation, page banner design and Like integration, and Twitter account creation and banner design
- a monthly work report, a monthly Google Analytics report and a Google Search Console report, with WhatsApp and email support

No search engine submission line appears anywhere on the page.

## How to Compare SEO Cost in India Before You Sign

1. Shortlist agencies that print their plans and prices, so you can read the line items before any sales call.
2. Ask for the price with GST stated in writing.
3. Ask what every line item is: which directories, which classified sites, and what a submission puts on your site.
4. Before you accept a 3-month or 6-month contract, put Google's timeframe question to the agency.
5. Match the keyword cap to the pages you need to rank. The caps printed on these pages run from 5 keywords to 500.
6. If the plan names a monthly report, request a sample and check it against [what a useful SEO report contains](/blog/what-is-seo-report/).
7. Note the date: an SEO package price in India can change between checks. These prices were read on 16 October 2026, and Expert Web World's ₹5,000 offer ends on 31 December 2026.

The rupee figure is the last thing to compare, after the line items and the term. Take the questions on Google's hiring page to the first call.
