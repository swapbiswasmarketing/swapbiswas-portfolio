ChatGPT Go and Google AI Plus both cost **₹399 a month** when I read the two vendors' prices logged out from an Indian connection on 25 October 2026 (Cloudflare's trace returned `loc=IN`). The first difference a buyer weighing ChatGPT Go vs Gemini would notice is storage: Google AI Plus comes with **400 GB** across Gmail, Drive and Photos, and OpenAI's pricing table has no storage row at all.

The same pairing works one tier up, where ChatGPT Plus at ₹1,999 sits ₹49 above Google AI Pro at ₹1,950. Because the prices match, I would decide on the bundle each vendor attaches to the price, and the bundles differ more than the model names suggest: Google adds storage, Gmail and video credits, and OpenAI adds higher limits than Free on tools, uploads, images and memory, on the same GPT-6 Luna model and unlimited text chats that ChatGPT Free already has, with a pricing card that says Go "may include ads".

## ChatGPT Go vs Gemini at ₹399: Google AI Plus Row by Row

At ₹399, Google AI Plus moves you from the free plan's Flash-Lite model to Flash and adds 400 GB of storage, while ChatGPT Go keeps ChatGPT Free's model and raises its limits. Every cell below is from the vendor's own page, read on 25 October 2026.

| Row | ChatGPT Go, ₹399 | Google AI Plus, ₹399 |
|---|---|---|
| Model named on the plan | GPT-6 Luna: "Yes", as on Free (GPT-6 Sol: "No"); OpenAI's Go article says Think uses GPT-5.6 Luna, though the pricing table marks GPT-5.6 Luna "No" on Go | "Access to our Flash model" (the free plan lists Flash-Lite) |
| Usage against the free plan | "More messages with tools", "More uploads", "More image creation", "More voice chats", "Longer memory" (OpenAI's Go article gives Go the same voice limits as Free); everyday text chats are unlimited on Free and Go alike | "2x higher usage limits than Free-of-charge" |
| How limits reset | Not stated on the pricing page | Every five hours "until you reach your weekly limit", with AI credits to extend |
| Image generation | "Yes" (Free: "Limited"); image generation with Thinking: "No" | Image generation and editing is on the free plan already; AI Plus adds "More access to Nano Banana in Search" |
| Video generation | No video generation row in the pricing table | "access to more features like video generation", plus 200 Google Flow credits with Gemini Omni Flash |
| Cloud storage | No storage row in the pricing table | 400 GB across Gmail, Drive and Photos |
| Office and Google apps | Excel, Word, PowerPoint and Google Sheets extensions: "Limited" | "Gemini in Gmail, Vids and more" |
| Deep research | "Limited" | Deep Research is on the free plan, and AI Plus includes "Everything in free" |
| Ads | The Go card reads "This plan may include ads." | No ads line on the AI Plus card; the plan cards' only ad-related lines concern YouTube on AI Pro and Ultra |
| Yearly billing | None: OpenAI's Go article says it does not support annual billing for Go | No yearly price on Google's India plan page |
| Free route open in India | None for new sign-ups | Google's student plan, redeem by 31 December 2026 |

The ChatGPT column comes from the plan cards and comparison table on OpenAI's pricing page and from [OpenAI's ChatGPT Go article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11989085-what-is-chatgpt-go), which says "Free and Go users both have unlimited everyday text chats" and that Go raises limits on "image generation, file uploads, and data analysis". The Google column is from the AI Plus card and footnotes on the India plan page. The pricing page leaves out when ChatGPT limits reset, so [how ChatGPT's limits and reset times work on each plan](/blog/chatgpt-usage-limits/) fills in the ChatGPT side of that row from OpenAI's other pages.

The rows I would weigh first at this price:

- Storage has no ChatGPT counterpart. If your Google account is near its free 15 GB, AI Plus is a storage upgrade with Gemini attached, and ChatGPT Go cannot do that job.
- Gemini inside Gmail starts at AI Plus, while Gemini inside Docs starts one tier up, at AI Pro. Google's AI Plus card names "Gmail, Vids and more", and its AI Pro card names "Gmail, Docs, Vids and more". Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM) limits also rise with each Google AI plan, and [the notebook and source caps per plan](/blog/notebooklm-pricing/) show how far AI Plus takes them.
- Ads are a ChatGPT-side question only. [OpenAI's ads FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001047-ads-in-chatgpt) says "Ads may appear for users on the Free and Go plans", and Google's plan cards have no ads line for the Gemini app. OpenAI's own pages disagree on whether Go shows ads today: the Go article says OpenAI "may start testing ads in ChatGPT Go in the future", while the pricing card already says it may include them. My [ChatGPT Go vs Plus comparison](/blog/chatgpt-go-vs-plus/) sets out that disagreement and which page to trust.

## ChatGPT Plus vs Google AI Pro at About ₹2,000

ChatGPT Plus costs ₹1,999 a month and Google AI Pro ₹1,950, so Google is ₹49 cheaper by the month and much cheaper by the year, because Plus has no yearly plan and AI Pro does.

| Row | ChatGPT Plus, ₹1,999 | Google AI Pro, ₹1,950 |
|---|---|---|
| Model named on the plan | GPT-6 Sol: "Yes"; "Advanced reasoning models with GPT-6" | "Access to Gemini 4 Argon", "our Pro model" and "our Flash model" |
| Usage | "Expanded messages and uploads" | "4x higher usage limits than Free-of-charge" |
| Images and video | Image generation with Thinking: "Yes"; no video generation row | 1,000 Google Flow credits with Gemini Omni Flash, and video generation |
| Cloud storage | No storage row | 5 TB across Gmail, Drive and Photos |
| Office and Google apps | Excel, Word, PowerPoint and Google Sheets extensions: "Yes" | "Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Vids and more" |
| Deep research | "Expanded deep research" | Deep Research, and "Higher access to Gemini 4 Argon" in Search |
| Coding | Codex: "Yes" | "Entry rate limits to agent model in Google Antigravity" |
| Extras | Sites: "Yes" (Go: "No"); interactive tables and charts: "Yes" (Go: "No") | YouTube Premium Lite plan |
| Ads | No ads line on the Plus card | The YouTube Premium Lite line is the card's only ad-related line |
| Yearly billing | None: the pricing page's FAQ offers annual plans "for Business and Enterprise" only | ₹19,500 a year on [Google One's plans page](https://one.google.com/about/plans), shown as "save ₹3,900" |
| Free route in India | None on OpenAI's India pricing page | Jio's 18-month offer for eligible Jio users, closed to new claims on 29 October 2026 |

The yearly row is where the prices separate. Twelve monthly Plus payments come to ₹23,988, and a year of AI Pro bought on Google's yearly plan is ₹19,500 (the same figure as Google AI Ultra 20x's monthly price, by coincidence), which is **₹4,488 less** (my arithmetic on the two vendors' prices). Google's India plan page also says "Cancel at any time. No refunds for partial billing periods, except as required by applicable law", so a yearly plan is a year's commitment in practice.

If your alternative at this price is Claude rather than Gemini, [the Claude vs ChatGPT comparison](/blog/claude-vs-chatgpt/) sets Claude Pro against ChatGPT Plus.

The top tiers pair less cleanly. On 25 October 2026:

- Google's other Ultra price, ₹6,500 a month for "5x higher usage limits vs. AI Pro", falls between ChatGPT Plus and Pro 100, with no ChatGPT plan at the same price.
- Pro 100 and Pro 500, priced in the ChatGPT price list linked under the lead table, have no Google plan at the same price.
- [OpenAI's Pro card](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) offers "Your choice of 3 usage tiers" with "Pro reasoning powered by GPT-6 Astra". [Google's Ultra card](https://gemini.google/in/subscriptions/?hl=en-IN) lists "Highest access to Gemini 4 Argon", Project Genie, a YouTube Premium individual plan and "Cloud storage starting at 20 TB".

## ChatGPT vs Gemini: Which Is Better?

On published features and prices, neither is better across the board: at the same ₹399, Google AI Plus lists 400 GB of storage, the Flash model and Gemini inside Gmail, while ChatGPT Go lists higher limits than Free on images, uploads and memory (GPT-6 Luna and unlimited everyday text chats are already on Free). Which is better for you depends on which of those rows you will use each week; this comparison is from the two plan pages only and runs no same-prompt test.

If the model name is what you are buying, the matched pair is ChatGPT Plus and Google AI Pro: [Google's India plan page](https://gemini.google/in/subscriptions/?hl=en-IN) puts Gemini 4 Argon on AI Pro and Ultra under the label "Includes Gemini 4 Argon", and [OpenAI's pricing table](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) starts GPT-6 Sol at Plus. Whether AI Plus also unlocks Google's Pro model is a point Google's own pages disagree on; [my Internshala and Google AI Plus comparison](/blog/internshala-google-ai-plus/) quotes both sides.

## Free Routes Still Open in India

Before paying ₹399, check the free routes, as of 25 October 2026:

- ChatGPT Go free: closed to new sign-ups. [OpenAI's release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes) say of the India offer, "as of January 21, 2026, the promo has ended". If you redeemed it, [what happens when the free Go year ends](/blog/chatgpt-go-free-india/) covers the renewal charge from 4 November 2026.
- Google AI Plus for students: [Google's India student page](https://gemini.google/in/students/?hl=en-IN) offers a one-year AI Plus student plan at no cost to eligible university students aged 18 and above, "Redeem until 31 December 2026", after which it charges ₹399 a month unless cancelled. [My guide to Google's Gemini student offer](/blog/gemini-student-offer/) covers who qualifies, the SheerID check and the claim steps.
- Google AI Pro through Jio: closed to new claims. Jio's offer period ended on 29 October 2026, and on 31 October 2026 [Jio's terms page](https://www.jio.com/jcms/en-in/jio-google-gemini-terms-and-conditions/) read "The Google Gemini Offer Is No Longer Available"; [the Jio Gemini Pro offer guide](/blog/jio-gemini-pro-offer/) covers when claimed plans end and what month 19 costs.

## Google AI Plus Since Its India Launch

Reports written when AI Plus launched in India describe a different plan from the one on sale now. [ETV Bharat's launch report of 10 December 2025](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/technology/google-ai-plus-plan-in-india-price-features-enn25121004850) listed:

| Detail | At launch, per ETV Bharat (10 December 2025) | On Google's India plan page, 25 October 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Price | ₹399 a month, or ₹199 a month for the first six months for new users | ₹399 a month, with no introductory price shown |
| Storage | 200 GB | 400 GB |
| Model | Gemini 3 Pro | "Access to our Flash model" |
| Google apps | Gemini in "Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and more" | "Gemini in Gmail, Vids and more" |

So a comparison written at launch understates the storage, and its model line no longer matches Google's plan card, which now names only Flash for AI Plus (Google's help article still lists Pro).
