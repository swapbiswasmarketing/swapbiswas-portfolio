## Meta Verified for Business Price: Introductory Offer vs Standard Rate

Meta's [19 July 2024 India announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/07/launching-meta-verified-subscription-plans-for-businesses-on-instagram-and-facebook-in-india/) prints a rupee range for business plans, and I found no later Meta page that does. What it says:

- Four plans: Meta Verified Business Standard, Business Plus, Business Premium and Business Max.
- Every plan carries the verified badge, account support and impersonation protection. Support "starts with access to chat or email with agents and builds to requesting a call back from the agent to dedicated case monitoring", and higher plans add more links in Reels.
- In India the plans were "available for purchase only via iOS or Android at this time", for Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp, or as a bundle for Facebook and Instagram together.
- Prices: "start at as little as INR 639 for a single app per month and go up to INR 21,000 with a 12-month introductory offer for two apps per month." After 12 months, standard rates start at INR 639 for a single app and go "up to INR 30,000 for two apps per month."
- Sign-up runs from the professional dashboard in the app or Meta's marketing landing page. That landing page redirected a logged-out request to Facebook's login page on 4 November 2026.

The arithmetic on Meta's own figures:

| | One app, entry price | Two apps, top price |
|---|---|---|
| Introductory offer, first 12 months | ₹639 a month | ₹21,000 a month |
| Standard rate, after 12 months | ₹639 a month | ₹30,000 a month |
| Change | None | ₹9,000 a month more, about 43% |

The post leaves several things unsaid: which of the four plans costs ₹21,000 or ₹30,000, the price of Plus or Premium, whether GST is included (the word does not appear on the page), and whether a business signing up in November 2026 still gets the introductory rate. A headline that gives ₹21,000 as the top price is quoting the first-year price.

## Meta Verified Price for Creators in India

Meta's 7 June 2023 India post, linked in the source table above, is the source of the ₹699 figure, and it is a creator launch:

- "People can purchase a monthly subscription for ₹699 on iOS and Android", with "a web purchase option for ₹599 a month" promised "in the coming months" and a waitlist in the Facebook web Account Center.
- The bundle: a verified badge after a government ID check, proactive monitoring for impersonation, and account support in English, with Hindi promised later.
- Eligibility: applicants at least 18 years old, accounts meeting "minimum activity requirements, such as prior posting history", and a government ID matching the profile name and photo. A footnote adds: "Businesses are not eligible to apply for Meta Verified at this time."
- Accounts verified before Meta Verified launched keep their badge.

The current creator price is harder to pin down. Meta's help articles "Subscribe to Meta Verified for creators" on [Facebook Help](https://www.facebook.com/help/734864594628372) and [Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/732454618598400) build their text with JavaScript and returned no readable price, and the India store rows say "Standard" without saying creator or business. I found no current rupee price for the creator plan on a Meta page, so the price your Instagram or Facebook app shows at checkout is the one to trust.

## Is Meta Verified Worth It for a Small Business in India?

It is worth paying for when you need what every plan includes, because Meta's July 2024 post lists the same base in all four: the verified badge, account support and impersonation protection. The same post says the badge "continues to be one of the top reported reasons for subscribing to Meta Verified."

Decision rules from Meta's own feature lists:

- Accounts being copied by impersonators, or stuck on account issues with no one to contact, are the cases the base plan covers.
- If the only goal is a badge on a personal Facebook profile, Facebook Verified is free where Meta has rolled it out.
- For a two-app bundle at the top of the range, budget for the standard rate, since Meta's post says the introductory price ends after 12 months.
- A monthly subscription nobody reviews is the cost I argue against in [my case for a five-tool marketing stack](/blog/marketing-tools-you-need/). Put a review date on Meta Verified the day you subscribe.
