## Can You Pay for ElevenLabs in Rupees or UPI?

Yes: ElevenLabs' help center says customers in India can pay in Indian rupees and can pay by UPI, with the same plans as everyone else. The INR article, quoted line by line:

- "Yes, customers located in India can now pay for their ElevenLabs subscription in Indian Rupees (INR)."
- "Customers in all other countries will continue to be charged in USD."
- "We also support UPI as a payment method for customers located in India."
- The plans do not change: "only the billing currency and available payment methods change based on your location".

ElevenLabs' [accepted payments article](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/account/payment-billing/what-kind-of-payment-is-accepted) lists four methods: credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay and "UPI (India only)". It also says a prepaid card "is not something we directly support". The FAQ on the pricing page gives a shorter list, "We accept Credit Card, Apple Pay, and Google Pay", with no UPI, so the two ElevenLabs pages disagree and the checkout screen is the one to believe.

Some India guides still say the opposite. [Zoutons' May 2026 guide to ElevenLabs pricing in India](https://www.zoutons.com/news/elevenlabs-pricing-india-may-2026) says "UPI is not currently supported" and converts the dollar prices at ₹84 to the dollar. ElevenLabs' own help center said otherwise on 25 October 2026.

The only rupee figures I found on an ElevenLabs page are Pay As You Go amounts (top-up limits and Auto Top Up defaults) for the prepaid balance that runs after your plan's credits are used. ElevenLabs' [Pay As You Go documentation](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/administration/pay-as-you-go) lists USD and INR as the supported currencies:

The minimum and the default imply 100 rupees a dollar and the caps imply 88, so neither tells you what a plan will cost at the INR checkout.

## Is ElevenLabs Free?

Yes: the Free plan costs $0 and gives **10,000 credits a month**, about 10 minutes of text to speech. Its limits, from the pricing page and ElevenLabs' [billing documentation](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/administration/billing):

- 3 Projects in Studio, against 20 on Starter.
- 3 custom voice slots for Voice Design. The billing page says "Cloning is only available on the Starter tier and above."
- Licence: "If you are on the free plan, you can use the content non-commercially with attribution." Starter adds a commercial licence.
- No rollover: the [credit rollover article](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/account/general/how-does-credit-rollover-work) says "credit rollover does not apply to accounts on the free tier".
- Top-ups: Pay As You Go is open to the Free plan, but the PAYG page says plan limits such as voice slots "come from your subscription plan, not your PAYG balance".

A free account suits a test of voices and pacing. A voiceover for a client, an ad or a monetised channel needs Starter or above for the commercial licence.

## Annual Billing, Rollover and Downgrade Rules

ElevenLabs states these rules on its pricing page and in its help center:

| Rule | In ElevenLabs' words | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Annual renewal | "Annual plans are billed on an annual cycle and will renew automatically unless cancelled." | [Annual or quarterly plans](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/account/payment-billing/do-you-offer-annual-or-quarterly-plans) |
| Quarterly plans | "At the moment, we do not offer quarterly plans." | Same article |
| Annual to monthly | "you can't upgrade from an annual subscription to a monthly subscription" | [Upgrading an annual plan](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/account/payment-billing/can-i-upgrade-my-annual-subscription) |
| Rollover | Unused credits carry over, "up to twice your usual subscription credit quota", on top of the new month's credits, so a Starter balance tops out at "30,000 x 3 = 90,000" | [Credit rollover](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/account/general/how-does-credit-rollover-work) |
| Upgrade | "Any unused credits from your previous plan will be added to your new credit quota." | [Upgrade or downgrade](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/account/general/what-happens-to-my-credits-when-i-upgrade-or-downgrade-my-subscription) |
| Downgrade or cancel | Takes effect at the end of the cycle, and "you will lose any unused credits on your account when this happens" | Same article |
| Top-up credits | Pay As You Go credits "will expire 12 months after purchase" and sit outside the rollover cap | [Credit rollover](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/account/general/how-does-credit-rollover-work) |

Start on monthly billing if you might want a smaller plan within the year: the rollover article says a downgrade or cancellation of an annual plan "does not take effect until the end of your current annual billing cycle".
