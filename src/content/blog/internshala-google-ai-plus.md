The Internshala Google AI Plus offer and Google's own one-year student plan are two free routes to Google AI Plus for a college student in India, and SheerID verification decides between them: Google One Help says Google verifies students through SheerID ["using a valid school email address"](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/17422238?hl=en-IN), while the pages describing Internshala's route base eligibility on your Internshala profile and never mention SheerID. Either way, [Google's India plan page](https://gemini.google/in/subscriptions/?hl=en-IN) prices AI Plus at ₹399 a month, and the plan renews at that price from month 13 unless you cancel.

As of 16 October 2026, [studentoffers.co](https://www.studentoffers.co/offer/google-ai-plus-x-internshala) says Internshala gives India college students Google AI Plus free for 12 months, then ₹399 a month unless cancelled. Google's own one-year student plan has [public terms](https://one.google.com/offer/studentoffer8?g1_landing_page=0), last updated 19 August 2026: SheerID verification, age 18+, and redemption "by December 31, 2026".

### How to Claim Google AI Plus Through Internshala

I have not seen the logged-in flow, so these steps come from the descriptions of two walkthroughs, by [Tube Rise](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-3qESV1Puu4) on 3 August 2026 and [Build With Akshat](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZRqsQPe6LaE) on 10 August 2026:

1. Create or log in to an Internshala student account.
2. Complete your Internshala profile. Build With Akshat's description says eligibility "can depend on your Internshala account, graduation details, region and other offer conditions".
3. Open the offer and claim the 12 months of Google AI Plus.
4. Redeem it through Google and add a payment method.
5. Before you confirm, look for the free year on Google's screen: "Always make sure Google shows the 12-month free trial before completing the subscription."
6. After activation, turn off renewal if you will not pay from month 13. Both descriptions list disabling AutoPay as a step.

## Google's Own Student Plan: One Year of AI Plus, Redeem by 31 December 2026

Google's India student page offers university students aged 18+ a "one-year Google AI Plus student plan" at no cost, with student status verified through SheerID. The 12 months run from the day you redeem, the offer terms say it "must be redeemed by 31 December 2026", and from month 13 it charges ₹399 a month unless you cancel. Who qualifies, the claim steps and the rules on switching from another trial are in [my guide to Google's Gemini student offer](/blog/gemini-student-offer/).

## Is Google AI Plus the Same as Gemini Pro?

No: Google AI Plus is the plan below Google AI Pro, with half of AI Pro's Gemini usage limits, 400 GB instead of 5 TB and no Gemini 4 Argon. Google's pages disagree on whether it reaches the Pro model: [Google's help article on Gemini models](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/18648722?hl=en-IN) lists Flash-Lite, Flash and Pro as the chat models on AI Plus, while Google's plan pages do not. The same article says higher-tier models "marked with a lock icon require an eligible plan upgrade", so the model menu in your own account shows which applies to you.

What Google lists for the two plans, read on 16 October 2026:

| Where Google says it | Google AI Plus | Google AI Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Chat models, help article on Gemini models | Flash-Lite, Flash and Pro | Flash-Lite, Flash, Pro and Argon |
| Task mode models, same article | N/A | Flash and Argon |
| Plan card, India plan page | "Access to our Flash model" | "Access to Gemini 4 Argon", "Access to our Pro model" and "Access to our Flash model" |
| Models, [Google One's plans FAQ](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/?hl=en-IN) | "Gemini 3.6 Flash and limited access to Gemini Omni Flash" | "expanded limits for 3.1 Pro", plus Gemini 4 Argon |
| "Access to our Pro models" row, Google One's comparison chart | A cross | A tick |
| Usage limits, [Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?hl=en-IN) | "2x higher than standard limits" | "4x higher than standard limits" |
| Context window, Gemini Apps Help | **128K tokens** | 1 million tokens |
| Storage, India plan page | 400 GB | 5 TB |
| Google apps, India plan page | "Gemini in Gmail, Vids and more" | "Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Vids and more" |

If Pro is unlocked on your account, the help article adds that "More advanced models and higher thinking levels require more of your usage", and on paid plans AI Plus gets half of AI Pro's usage (2x the standard limits against 4x), so save Pro for the prompts that need it. Notebook caps differ too: on AI Plus, Gemini Notebook (the tool formerly called NotebookLM) allows 200 notebooks and 100 sources per notebook, against 500 and 300 on AI Pro, per [the Gemini Notebook caps by plan](/blog/notebooklm-pricing/).

Pro wording turns up on pages about the Internshala offer:

- Vishwa Mohan's LinkedIn post says the offering "includes access to Gemini Pro capabilities".
- studentoffers.co describes "Gemini app with Pro access".
- coursejoiner.com lists "Gemini 3.1 Pro" under "Primary Features Included".
- A 9 August walkthrough by [Tricky Man](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gIjYkTmU17o), a channel that calls coursejoiner.com its official website, lists "Google AI Pro Subscription Benefits" in its description, including "5TB Google Cloud Storage".

The first three fit the help article's list of AI Plus chat models. The fourth describes AI Pro: 5 TB is the AI Pro storage figure, and AI Plus lists 400 GB.

If you need the AI Pro plan itself as a student, Google One Help lists a separate offer: "a Google AI Pro plan at a discounted rate while they remain a student, for up to four consecutive years", verified through SheerID the same way. The public India pages I read do not print its price. Jio's route to AI Pro for customers on eligible plans closed to new claims when its offer period ended on 29 October 2026, and [my guide to Jio's Gemini Pro offer](/blog/jio-gemini-pro-offer/) covers that offer and lists Google's India prices from Free to Ultra. ## Google AI Plus After 12 Months: Renewal and How to Cancel

On Google's route, the offer terms say your payment method is charged the standard monthly price for your country until you cancel, which the India student page puts at ₹399 a month, and Google One Help says you are notified before the change. For Internshala's route, coursejoiner.com says Google sends a reminder 7 days before the trial ends.

Before you let it renew, I would set it against ChatGPT Go, which costs the same each month; [my Go and AI Plus comparison for India](/blog/chatgpt-go-vs-gemini/) lists what each plan adds over its free tier.

To cancel, [Google One Help's cancel article](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9056360?hl=en-IN) gives these steps:

1. On a computer, go to Google One.
2. Click Settings, then Cancel membership.
3. Click Cancel membership again to confirm.

What cancelling does, in Google's words:

| Question | Google's wording |
|---|---|
| Do I keep the free year? | The offer terms say your membership "may remain active until the end of the Offer Period" |
| When do paid benefits stop? | "You will have until the end of the billing cycle to use them", per the cancel article |
| What storage do I keep? | "Each person will keep their default storage of up to 15 GB at no charge." |
| What if I use more than that? | You "will not be able to store anything new" until you delete files or buy a plan, per the offer terms |

Once a cancelled plan runs out, you are back on Gemini's free plan: the India plan page lists it with "Access to our Flash-Lite model", and Gemini Apps Help gives it a 32K-token context window, against 128K on AI Plus. [My list of free AI tools for marketing work](/blog/free-ai-tools-for-marketing/) includes other no-cost assistants. If you are deciding between keeping AI Plus and paying for Claude instead, compare [what Claude Pro costs in India](/blog/claude-pro-price-in-india/) before the free year ends.
