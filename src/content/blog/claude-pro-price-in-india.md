## Claude India Pricing Not Showing? What the Public Page Does

Logged out, Anthropic's public pricing page shows dollars to a visitor in India, so a missing rupee price there matches what I recorded on 10 October 2026.

Where a rupee (INR) price for your account would appear:

- Anthropic's Pro plan article names two places: claude.ai/upgrade and your mobile app store.
- If you already subscribe and your bill is in dollars, Anthropic's Pro and Max invoices article has the rule: "Some subscribers pay their subscriptions using a different currency than the currency (or currencies) new subscribers would be offered in their region." To change it, you mark the plan to cancel at the end of its term, wait for the term to end and buy the plan again.
- Logged out, claude.ai/upgrade redirects to the login page, so the price for an account sits behind a login and this post cannot show it.
- claude.com/en-in/pricing and claude.com/in/pricing both returned a 404 on the same date, so there is no separate India pricing page at those addresses.

I loaded claude.com/pricing in a clean Chrome profile with no Claude login, on an Indian connection (Cloudflare's trace returned loc=IN). The page showed $17 a month for Pro with $200 "billed up front", $20 "if billed monthly", "From $100" for Max, a footnote saying the prices shown exclude applicable tax, and no rupee sign anywhere.

That page loads a script, [webflow-local-pricing.js](https://claude.com/shared/webflow-local-pricing.js), whose header comment calls it "Localized subscription pricing for claude.com pages". The script asks one endpoint, `https://claude.ai/api/billing/subscription_pricing`, for prices by the visitor's country, and its notes say "Server-rendered USD prices remain visible until the fetch resolves" and "If the fetch fails, USD stays."

That endpoint is undocumented and can change without notice. Requested from the same Indian connection on 10 October 2026, it returned:

| Field in the response | Value returned for India |
|---|---|
| Country | IN |
| Currency | USD |
| Pro, monthly | 2000 minor units, which is $20 |
| Pro, annual | 20000 minor units, which is $200 |
| Max 5x and Max 20x (Max and Ultra since the rename), monthly | 10000 and 20000 minor units, which is $100 and $200 |
| Tax shown as included | false |

Asked for the United States, it returned the same amounts. Asked for Germany, through the `?country=DE` test override described in the script's header, it returned euros. So the endpoint carries local-currency prices for at least one other country, and on this date it answered India in dollars.

Some vendors do geo-serve rupees on a public pricing page. In my 7 September 2026 capture of SaaS pricing pages for [a tier-name audit](/blog/pricing-tier-names/), four pages came back in Indian rupees: Slack, Mailchimp, Stripe and Grammarly.

## Claude Pro Price in India Yearly: The Annual Plan Arithmetic

Apple's India listing prices the annual plan at ₹19,900, which is **₹4,088 less** than twelve monthly payments of ₹1,999.

| Checkout | Twelve monthly payments | Annual price | Annual plan saves |
|---|---|---|---|
| App Store, India | 12 x ₹1,999 = ₹23,988 | ₹19,900 | ₹4,088 (17.0%) |
| Web, as reported in July 2026 | 12 x ₹2,399 = ₹28,788 | ₹24,000 | ₹4,788 (16.6%) |
| Anthropic's dollar list price | 12 x $20 = $240 | $200 | $40 (16.7%) |
| App Store, US | 12 x $20.00 = $240.00 | $214.99 | $25.01 (10.4%) |

The US row comes from [Apple's US listing for Claude](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/claude-by-anthropic/id6473753684) on 10 October 2026. What the table means for a buyer in India:

- On the App Store, ten monthly payments come to ₹19,990, so the annual plan is ahead from the tenth month. On the July web figures, ten payments come to ₹23,990 against ₹24,000, so it is ahead from the eleventh.
- Annual billing is a prepayment. The FAQ on Anthropic's pricing page says "you're billed once up front for the year" and that payments are "generally non-refundable", with exceptions in its consumer terms and in local law.
- Switching later is allowed from Settings > Billing. Anthropic's [annual billing article](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/10185996-how-to-change-your-pro-plan-from-monthly-to-annual-billing) says unused monthly time "will be automatically credited to your first annual invoice", so the lower-risk order is monthly first, then annual once you know you will keep the plan for most of a year.
- Max and Ultra have no annual option. The Max plan article says the plan is "currently available as a monthly subscription only", and the Ultra plan article says the same of Ultra.

Student pricing is a separate question, covered in [the routes open to a student in India](/blog/claude-student-discount-india/).
