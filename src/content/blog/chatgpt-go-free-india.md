If you got ChatGPT Go free in India, the earliest your free year can run out is **4 November 2026**: OpenAI's [India promotion article](https://web.archive.org/web/20251104165608/https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12739021-chatgpt-go-promotion-india) opened sign-ups on 4 November 2025 and pushed each first bill back by 12 months. On 5 November 2026, that article still redirected to OpenAI's main ChatGPT Go article, and neither the Go plan page nor the pricing page mentioned a free year.

**Update, 5 November 2026:** OpenAI's help center now has an article on [ChatGPT Go India trial renewals and continuation offers](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001631-chatgpt-go-india-trial-renewals-and-continuation-offers). It says your free trial "ends on the date shown for your subscription", and a subscription set to renew "continues as a paid monthly subscription unless you cancel". Some accounts may be shown a continuation offer, a discounted monthly price for 3 paid billing periods and then ₹399 a month, on the web and in the Android app but not in the iOS app. The offer "does not restart the original free year".

Look up your trial end date on your own billing page or app store subscription today and choose Free, Go or Plus before it arrives, because on that date the default is an automatic charge. Free is a closer substitute than it was when you signed up: since August 2026, OpenAI has given Free users the same unlimited everyday text chats as Go.

## Is ChatGPT Go Still Free in India?

No, not for new sign-ups. OpenAI gives no exact last date, but its [release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes), read on 5 November 2026, say that as of 21 January 2026 the India promotion had ended. Accounts that redeemed keep Go free until their trial end date, then renew at the current Go fee (₹399 a month on the web) unless they cancel or accept a continuation offer.

## How to Cancel ChatGPT Go Autopay Where You Paid

OpenAI's [cancel article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7232927-canceling-your-chatgpt-subscription), read on 5 November 2026, says to cancel "through the platform where you purchased it":

| Where you paid | Steps in OpenAI's cancel article |
|---|---|
| chatgpt.com, by card or UPI | Sign in, open your account menu, select Settings, select Billing, then under Cancel plan select Cancel |
| Apple App Store | On the iPhone or iPad, open Settings, select your name, then Subscriptions, then ChatGPT, then Cancel Subscription, and confirm |
| Google Play | In the Play Store app, open Subscriptions, select ChatGPT, then Cancel subscription; on the web, open your Google Play subscriptions, select Manage next to ChatGPT, then Cancel subscription |

From the same article:

- "Cancel at least 24 hours before your next billing date to avoid being charged for the next subscription period."
- "Uninstalling the ChatGPT app does not cancel your subscription."
- "Canceling stops future renewals. It does not automatically refund previous charges."
- Deleting your ChatGPT account cancels a subscription billed directly by ChatGPT, but "It does not cancel Apple App Store subscriptions."
- "A subscription purchased on chatgpt.com does not appear in your Apple or Google Play subscriptions."

OpenAI's pages differ on the menu and on what you keep after cancelling:

- Where billing sits on the web: as of November 2026 the Go article and the cancel article both say Settings, then Billing; only the Go article's answer on switching plans still says profile icon, then Settings, then Account. Look under Billing first.
- Access after cancelling a free promotion: the archived India promotion article said "you will lose access to ChatGPT Go at the end of your current 30-day billing cycle", and the India trial renewals article says "If you canceled but still have Go access, your current subscription has not yet ended." OpenAI's [promotions-and-referrals article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12501659-chatgpt-go-promotions-and-referrals), read on 5 November 2026 and written for partner promo codes, says "If it was a free promotion, your access may end immediately upon cancellation." The cancel article says that after an ordinary cancellation "paid features remain available until the end of the current billing period".

When to cancel, if you want every free month: in the last few days before your renewal date, and at least 24 hours before it. Cancelling then keeps you clear of both promotion rules above: under the archived India promotion article you keep Go to the end of that cycle, and if the promotions-and-referrals article's "may end immediately" rule applies instead, you lose a few days at most. If a continuation offer appears while you cancel on the web or Android, the India trial renewals article says "You can accept the offer shown or continue canceling."

### If You Paid by UPI, Cancel the AutoPay Mandate Too

Google Pay's help center describes recurring UPI payments, including "Subscriptions and other merchant requests", as UPI AutoPay mandates that you approve in the app. Its [steps to revoke a live mandate](https://support.google.com/pay/india/answer/10840624?hl=en) are: tap your profile picture, select Autopay, tap the mandate in the "Live" tab, choose "Cancel autopay" and enter your UPI PIN.

- What revoking does: "After you revoke a mandate, merchants can't debit the money from your account for that mandate," per Google Pay's [Manage AutoPay page](https://support.google.com/pay/india/answer/10452280?hl=en).
- The warning you get: "You'll get a notification 24 hours before any debit from the bank," on the same page. Do not wait for it to decide, since OpenAI's own cut-off is also 24 hours before the billing date.
- If the mandate sits in PhonePe or Paytm: PhonePe's [article on subscription mandates](https://www.phonepe.com/blog/trust-and-safety/how-to-identify-fraudulent-subscription-mandates/) says to go to your "PhonePe Profile > Autopay Settings and delete the mandate", and Paytm's [guide to deactivating UPI AutoPay](https://paytm.com/blog/bill-payments/upi-autopay/deactivate-upi-autopay-paytm/) goes from your profile icon to "UPI & Payment Settings", then "Automatic UPI Payments", where you pick the mandate and cancel it (both read on 16 October 2026).
- Lost track of the app that holds the mandate: NPCI says its MyUPI platform, launched on 10 September 2026, "provides users with a single view of UPI transactions and AutoPay mandates across banks and UPI apps" ([NPCI's announcement](https://www.npci.org.in/uploads/NPCI_Press_Release_RBI_Governor_Unveils_New_UPI_Capabilities_at_GFF_2026_AI_Powered_Customer_Support_and_Seamless_Tap_and_Pay_Experience_bb763a7670.pdf)).

## ChatGPT Go Free in India: Decide Before the Renewal Date

For anyone who got ChatGPT Go free in India, the 4 November 2026 above is only the earliest renewal. The date that decides your charge is the one on your own billing page or store subscription, and prices, plans and terms can change before it arrives, so read that page on the day you choose Free, Go or Plus. If a continuation offer appears, check its monthly price and the date of its first payment before you accept it.

Set a calendar reminder two days before your renewal date.
