If you got ChatGPT Go free in India, the earliest your free year can run out is **4 November 2026**: OpenAI's [India promotion article](https://web.archive.org/web/20251104165608/https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12739021-chatgpt-go-promotion-india) opened sign-ups on 4 November 2025 and pushed each first bill back by 12 months. On 16 October 2026, that article redirected to OpenAI's main ChatGPT Go article, and neither the Go plan page nor the pricing page mentioned a free year.

Look up your renewal date on your own billing page or app store subscription this week and choose Free, Go or Plus before it arrives, because on that date the default is an automatic charge. Free is a closer substitute than it was when you signed up: since August 2026, OpenAI has given Free users the same unlimited everyday text chats as Go.

## Is ChatGPT Go Still Free in India?

No, not for new sign-ups. OpenAI gives no exact last date, but its [release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes), read on 16 October 2026, say that as of 21 January 2026 the India promotion had ended. Accounts that redeemed keep Go free until their 12 months end, then renew at the current Go fee (₹399 a month on the web) unless they cancel.

|---|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT website, by card or UPI | 4 November 2025 | 12 months after you redeemed, so 4 November 2026 at the earliest | OpenAI, at the current Go fee; ChatGPT Settings, then Billing |
| Google Play, which required you to be "physically located in India" with your Play account location set to India | 4 November 2025 | 12 months after you redeemed, so 4 November 2026 at the earliest | Google Play, in your local currency; Play Store, then Subscriptions |

| Already a Go subscriber on Apple, or on Plus or Pro | You had to cancel, wait out the paid period and redeem as a new user | 12 months after that redemption | The site or store you redeemed on; cancel there |

- The promotion had ended as of 21 January 2026, so the last free years run out by 21 January 2027.
- On the web, the plan and its dates sit under Settings in ChatGPT; a store subscription sits in the App Store's or Google Play's subscriptions page.
- If you redeemed with UPI, the India promotion article warned of a temporary charge of "INR (₹) 1 on your account every billing basis" that "will be refunded". ## Does ChatGPT Go Show Ads in India?

It can. OpenAI's [ads FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001047-ads-in-chatgpt), updated 14 October 2026, says "Ads may appear for users on the Free and Go plans", and [TechCrunch reported on 27 August 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/27/openai-to-start-showing-ads-on-chatgpts-free-and-go-tiers-in-india/) that "OpenAI said on Thursday that it will start showing ads on its ChatGPT Free and Go subscription tiers in India." OpenAI's Go article, updated 12 October, still says only "We may start testing ads in ChatGPT Go in the future."

| Point | The ads FAQ's wording |
|---|---|
| Plans without ads | "Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu accounts will not have ads." |
| Where ads sit | "Ads can appear below the end of a response", and they are "clearly labeled as sponsored" |
| Ads-Free on Go | "Ads-Free is a Free-plan option. If you're on a paid plan, you'd need to switch back to Free to use it, or upgrade to a plan that's already ad-free (like Plus or Pro)." |
| The cost of Ads-Free on Free | "lower usage limits and reduced feature access (for example, fewer messages and no access to some tools like image generation or deep research)" |
| Chats that never show ads | "Temporary Chats will not show ads." |
| Users under 18 | "We do not show ads to accounts identified as belonging to people under 18." |

None of the OpenAI pages I read names India as a place where users see ads. The ads FAQ describes a rollout "to eligible users on the Free and Go plans in select regions" without listing them, and the release notes record rollouts for the US (9 February 2026), Australia, New Zealand and Canada (16 April 2026) and the UK (4 June 2026), with no India entry as of 16 October 2026. India is listed as "Available" on OpenAI's [Ads Manager availability page](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001245-ads-manager-availability), but that page covers advertisers based in India, not who sees ads.

## How to Cancel ChatGPT Go Autopay Where You Paid

OpenAI's [cancel article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7232927-canceling-your-chatgpt-subscription), updated 14 October 2026, says to cancel "through the platform where you purchased it":

| Where you paid | Steps in OpenAI's cancel article |
|---|---|
| chatgpt.com, by card or UPI | Sign in, open your account menu, select Settings, select Billing, then under Cancel plan select Cancel |
| Apple App Store | On the iPhone or iPad, open Settings, select your name, then Subscriptions, then ChatGPT, then Cancel Subscription, and confirm |
| Google Play | In the Play Store app, open Subscriptions, select ChatGPT, then Cancel subscription; on the web, open your Google Play subscriptions, select Manage next to ChatGPT, then Cancel subscription |

From the same article:

- "Cancel at least 24 hours before your next billing date to avoid being charged for the next subscription period."
- "Uninstalling the ChatGPT app does not cancel your subscription."
- "Canceling stops future renewals. It does not automatically refund previous charges."
- Deleting your ChatGPT account cancels a subscription billed directly by ChatGPT, but "It does not cancel Apple App Store subscriptions."
- "A subscription purchased on chatgpt.com does not appear in your Apple or Google Play subscriptions."

OpenAI's pages differ on the menu and on what you keep after cancelling:

- Where billing sits on the web: the Go article says profile icon, then Settings, then Account; the cancel article says Settings, then Billing. Look under both.
- Access after cancelling a free promotion: the archived India promotion article said "you will lose access to ChatGPT Go at the end of your current 30-day billing cycle". OpenAI's [promotions-and-referrals article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12501659-chatgpt-go-promotions-and-referrals), updated 29 July 2026 and written for partner promo codes, says "If it was a free promotion, your access may end immediately upon cancellation." The cancel article says that after an ordinary cancellation "paid features remain available until the end of the current billing period".

When to cancel, if you want every free month: in the last few days before your renewal date, and at least 24 hours before it. Cancelling then keeps you clear of both promotion rules above: under the archived India promotion article you keep Go to the end of that cycle, and if the promotions-and-referrals article's "may end immediately" rule applies instead, you lose a few days at most.

### If You Paid by UPI, Cancel the AutoPay Mandate Too

Google Pay's help center describes recurring UPI payments, including "Subscriptions and other merchant requests", as UPI AutoPay mandates that you approve in the app. Its [steps to revoke a live mandate](https://support.google.com/pay/india/answer/10840624?hl=en) are: tap your profile picture, select Autopay, tap the mandate in the "Live" tab, choose "Cancel autopay" and enter your UPI PIN.

- What revoking does: "After you revoke a mandate, merchants can't debit the money from your account for that mandate," per Google Pay's [Manage AutoPay page](https://support.google.com/pay/india/answer/10452280?hl=en).
- The warning you get: "You'll get a notification 24 hours before any debit from the bank," on the same page. Do not wait for it to decide, since OpenAI's own cut-off is also 24 hours before the billing date.
- If the mandate sits in PhonePe or Paytm: PhonePe's [article on subscription mandates](https://www.phonepe.com/blog/trust-and-safety/how-to-identify-fraudulent-subscription-mandates/) says to go to your "PhonePe Profile > Autopay Settings and delete the mandate", and Paytm's [guide to deactivating UPI AutoPay](https://paytm.com/blog/bill-payments/upi-autopay/deactivate-upi-autopay-paytm/) goes from your profile icon to "UPI & Payment Settings", then "Automatic UPI Payments", where you pick the mandate and cancel it (both read on 16 October 2026).
- Lost track of the app that holds the mandate: NPCI says its MyUPI platform, launched on 10 September 2026, "provides users with a single view of UPI transactions and AutoPay mandates across banks and UPI apps" ([NPCI's announcement](https://www.npci.org.in/uploads/NPCI_Press_Release_RBI_Governor_Unveils_New_UPI_Capabilities_at_GFF_2026_AI_Powered_Customer_Support_and_Seamless_Tap_and_Pay_Experience_bb763a7670.pdf)).
