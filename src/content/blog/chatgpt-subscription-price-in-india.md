The ChatGPT subscription price in India runs from **₹399 a month** for Go through ₹1,999 for Plus up to **₹52,900** for Pro 500, GST included, as I read OpenAI's prices on 16 October 2026. Take the 18% GST out and set each plan against its pre-tax US dollar price, and Go breaks the pattern: it works out to **₹42.27 per dollar**, while Plus, the three Pro tiers and Business all sit between **₹84.32 and ₹90.67 per dollar**.

I see Go as the outlier in OpenAI's India lineup, with every plan above it priced near its US price. At [RBI's reference rate](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/ReferenceRateArchive.aspx) for 9 October 2026, ₹96.6149 per dollar (tick USD and enter the date in its form), Go's India price before GST is about **44%** of its US price, and every other plan's is 87% to 94%.

Read logged out from India on 16 October 2026, ChatGPT costs ₹399 a month for Go, ₹1,999 for Plus and ₹10,699, ₹19,900 or ₹52,900 for Pro 100, 200 or 500, GST included. Business is ₹2,250 per user a month, or ₹1,800 a month billed annually, plus 18% GST. Web UPI covers Go and Plus only.

- The chart's reference line is RBI's ₹96.6149 per dollar for 9 October 2026. A plan on that line would cost the same in India before GST as in the US before sales tax.

## ChatGPT Go and Plus Price in India

Go is ₹399 a month and Plus ₹1,999 on the web and on Apple's India listing, as of 16 October 2026. Before 18% GST they are ₹338.14 and ₹1,694.07. Both accept UPI on the web, and both bill monthly.

- Go launched in India at that price. OpenAI's [release notes of 18 August 2025](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes#:~:text=geo%2Drestricted%20to%20India) introduced it "For ₹399/month (GST included)", for users in India only at first, payable "by credit card or UPI".
- Go is not free for new sign-ups: OpenAI's release notes say India's 12-month offer had ended as of 21 January 2026. If you redeemed it, [what happens when the free Go year ends](/blog/chatgpt-go-free-india/) covers the renewal charge and how to cancel.
- Google AI Plus costs the same each month as Go, and the bundles differ more than the prices do: [Go set against Google AI Plus row by row](/blog/chatgpt-go-vs-gemini/) covers models, storage, ads and Gmail.
- Plus costs ₹1,600 a month more than Go. What that buys, row by row from OpenAI's pricing table, is in [my ChatGPT Go vs Plus vs Pro comparison](/blog/chatgpt-go-vs-plus/), which works in US dollars.
- If the alternative is Claude, [ChatGPT Plus set against Claude Pro](/blog/claude-vs-chatgpt/) is its own comparison, and [Claude's rupee prices by checkout](/blog/claude-pro-price-in-india/) include a section on how ChatGPT Go compares.

OpenAI's own billing has no yearly Plus plan. Its [Plus article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6950777-what-is-chatgpt-plus) says: "Currently, we do not support annual billing or the option to pay for multiple months in advance for ChatGPT Plus subscriptions." The Go article says the same for "ChatGPT Go, Plus, or Pro", and the pricing page's FAQ reads: "We offer monthly plans for Go, Plus and Business and annual plans for Business and Enterprise."

|---|---|---|
| Go | 12 x ₹399 = ₹4,788 | Monthly, automatic |
| Plus | 12 x ₹1,999 = ₹23,988 | Monthly, automatic |

Two other figures can look like a yearly Plus price, and no page I read confirms either one as that:

- Apple's India listing carries a second "ChatGPT Plus" purchase at ₹19,900 beside the ₹1,999 one, and the listing does not say what period it covers. It is absent from the archived copies of 24 January, 3 May and 15 May 2025 and present in every copy I read from 15 June 2025 on. Check the billing period on Apple's purchase screen before buying it.
- The India file holds a Plus "year" entry of ₹1,665.83 a month. None of OpenAI's pages that I read offers that plan.

## ChatGPT Pro Price in India: Pro 100 vs Pro 200 vs Pro 500

ChatGPT Pro is three monthly plans, Pro 100, Pro 200 and Pro 500, priced at $100, $200 and $500 in OpenAI's [Pro tiers article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-about-chatgpt-pro-tiers) and at ₹10,699, ₹19,900 and ₹52,900 a month with GST in India, as of 16 October 2026.

The Pro 100 description is from the [9 April 2026 release note](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes#:~:text=New%20Pro%20plan%20options) that introduced the $100 plan, and the other two are from the Pro tiers article.

What the rupee prices and the article add up to for a buyer in India:

- The steps differ from the dollar steps because each tier sits at its own rate per dollar before GST: ₹90.67 for Pro 100, ₹84.32 for Pro 200 and ₹89.66 for Pro 500. Moving up from Pro 100 to Pro 200 costs relatively less in India than in the US, and moving from Pro 200 to Pro 500 relatively more.
- Pro 200 now comes with less usage for new subscribers. The Pro tiers article says new subscriptions "that aren't eligible for grandfathering include a lower usage allowance", while the monthly price stays the same.
- Grandfathering covers anyone with an active Pro 200 subscription at any point from 22 September 2026 through 10 a.m. Pacific Time on 29 September 2026. They keep the previous allowance "through October 29, 2026", and after that date the subscription "will move to the lower included usage allowance".
- Ultrafast is a Pro 500 feature: "Among Pro plans, Ultrafast is available only on Pro 500", and "At launch, buying credits on Pro 100 or Pro 200 does not unlock Ultrafast." It uses the included allowance at 8 times the Standard rate.
- No tier takes UPI on the web or bills yearly. The article says "Pro is billed monthly. Annual billing and payment for multiple months in advance are not available."
- Refunds follow the checkout. For App Store purchases the article says to "request a refund directly from Apple", and for chatgpt.com or Google Play purchases to contact OpenAI Support.

For what Pro adds over Plus, tier by tier, the Plus vs Pro section of the Go vs Plus vs Pro comparison sets it out in dollars.

## ChatGPT Business Price in India, per Seat

ChatGPT Business costs ₹1,800 per user a month billed annually, or ₹2,250 billed monthly, with a two-user minimum, on the logged-out pricing page on 16 October 2026. The India file marks both amounts tax-exclusive with GST at 18%, which makes them ₹2,124 and ₹2,655 a month once GST is added (my arithmetic).

| Smallest workspace, 2 users | Before GST | With 18% GST, if added at checkout |
|---|---|---|
| Billed monthly | ₹4,500 a month | ₹5,310 a month |
| Billed annually | ₹43,200 a year, paid up front | ₹50,976 a year |

From OpenAI's [Business billing article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8792536-managing-billing-and-seats-in-chatgpt-business) and the pricing page's FAQ:

- Annual billing is a commitment for the year. The article works its annual example as seats x $20 x 12 months, and says that "On annual plans, added seats remain committed through the end of the current annual term."
- Payment is by card. "ChatGPT Business supports credit and debit card payments", and invoice billing, bank transfer and purchase orders "are not available for self-serve Business workspaces". The multi-currency article rules out UPI for Business.
- On tax, the Go article sends "VAT or GST exemption" requests to this Business article, whose tax section says that "If you are in a region that levies VAT, you can self-identify as a business and enter your Tax ID at checkout." It names VAT and not GST. The India file also sets `show_reverse_charge_disclaimer` to true (the US file sets it to false), a flag that none of the OpenAI help articles I read explains. A GST-registered business should check at checkout whether a Tax ID field appears for India, and whether the total adds GST or shows a reverse-charge note.
- A Premium seat is listed in the article at $125 a month, or $100 billed annually, with "5x more usage than Standard seats". The logged-out India page showed no Premium price. The US file prices an entry named `business_prolite` at those same $125 and $100, and the India file prices it at ₹11,250 a month or ₹9,000 billed annually, before GST, which is again ₹90 per dollar. Neither file calls it Premium, so the match is mine, not a published India price.
- Nonprofits can get a discount: the pricing page's FAQ says "nonprofits can now access up to a 75% discount on ChatGPT Business or ChatGPT Enterprise" through OpenAI for Nonprofits.

## ChatGPT Price in India for Students

OpenAI's pricing page, as served in India on 16 October 2026, has no student price on either of its tabs, Individual (Free, Go, Plus and Pro) or Business & Enterprise.

- ChatGPT Edu is bought by universities. The pricing page's FAQ calls it "an affordable plan that lets universities deploy AI across their campus communities", and the [ChatGPT Edu page](https://chatgpt.com/business/education/) offers a "Contact sales" button in place of a price, so a student's route to Edu runs through their university.
- OpenAI's India education program also runs through institutions. Its [Learning Accelerator announcement](https://openai.com/global-affairs/learning-accelerator/) of 25 August 2025 planned to distribute "approximately half a million ChatGPT licenses and training to educators and students across India" over the following six months. Of the three partnerships it lists for that distribution, AICTE's was to give "ChatGPT access to technical institutes nationwide", and the other two were for school teachers. The page offers no way for a student to apply alone, so ask your institute whether it took part.
- OpenAI's student offer is for the US. Its [2026 student page](https://chatgpt.com/students/2026/) gives four free months of ChatGPT Plus, "An $80 value for eligible U.S. college students", to students "enrolled at eligible U.S. degree-granting institutions", and says "Claim by October 31."
- The pricing page also shows a pop-up banner reading "For life sciences research, explore the Rosalind Academic Plan". It links to [OpenAI's post of 15 October 2026](https://openai.com/index/introducing-the-rosalind-academic-plan/), which describes a plan for research labs: principal investigators and lab leads apply on behalf of a lab, and the plan "is currently available to academic labs at U.S. universities".
- The "Students" link in the pricing page's menu, served in India, opens [a page of example chats](https://chatgpt.com/use-cases/students-india/) by "More than 25 college students across India", with no price or offer on it.

A student in India buying ChatGPT individually pays the standard prices, starting at ₹399 for Go. Claude has a separate list: [the Claude student routes open in India](/blog/claude-student-discount-india/).

### Has the ChatGPT Price in India Gone Up?

Not on the two plans Apple's India listing carried in January 2025. In all 28 archived copies and on the live listing, Plus was ₹1,999 and a Pro item sat at ₹19,900, listed as ChatGPT Pro up to the 22 February 2026 copy, Pro 20x from the 29 March to the 28 September 2026 copies, and Pro 200 from the 29 September 2026 copy. OpenAI's archived Pro tiers article calls that plan "the ChatGPT Pro $200 plan (Pro 20X)".

Since January 2025, OpenAI has added plans (Go, Pro 100 and Pro 500) and given new Pro 200 subscriptions a lower allowance at the same price.

One of OpenAI's own pages lags behind these changes. On 16 October 2026 the multi-currency article still described "the pause on new ChatGPT Pro $200 (Pro 20X) subscriptions", which the 29 September release note had ended.

## ChatGPT Subscription Price in India: Recheck Before You Pay

The ChatGPT subscription price in India had one outlier on 16 October 2026: Go, at ₹42.27 per dollar before GST, against ₹84.32 to ₹90.67 for every other plan. Prices and allowances change, and OpenAI's pages do not always agree, so recheck these on the day you subscribe:

1. chatgpt.com/pricing, logged out, on both tabs, for Go, Plus, the Pro starting price and Business.
2. The India file at the address in the sources table, for the Pro 200 and Pro 500 web prices and the tax flags.
3. Apple's India listing, for the in-app prices and the second Plus item.
4. OpenAI's Pro tiers article after 29 October 2026, when grandfathered Pro 200 subscriptions move to the lower allowance.
5. The multi-currency billing article, for UPI by plan.

Last, log in and read the amount on your own upgrade screen, which shows the price for your account.
