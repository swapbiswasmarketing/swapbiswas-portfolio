A four-person marketing team on Framer Pro, with two extra editors, one content editor and one translation locale, pays $30 + $40 + $10 + $20 = **$100 a month** at yearly billing, by the Framer pricing page at [framer.com/pricing](https://www.framer.com/pricing) as I read it on 25 October 2026. That is **3.3 times** the $30 headline price of Pro, and the plan itself is under a third of the bill.

So the useful question about Framer's prices is what your bill adds up to; the plan you pick is only its first line. The plan sets your limits; editor seats, languages and overage blocks set what you pay. And if you open the page from India, it prices every line in rupees, at well under the dollar price converted at market rates.

## Framer Pricing Plans at a Glance

Framer sells four plans. The page defaults to yearly billing, so the headline prices below are monthly rates billed for a year at a time. The rupee column is what the page showed when I loaded it logged out from an Indian connection on 25 October 2026.

| Plan | Per month, yearly billing | Pages | CMS collections / items | Bandwidth | AI credits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 1,000 (per Framer's FAQ) | 10 collections | 1 GB | 500 to try |
| Basic | **$10** (₹361 from India) | 30, fixed | 2 / 1,000 | 50 GB | 1,000 a month |
| Pro | **$30** (₹1,084 from India) | 150, then $20 per 100 (700 max) | 10 / 2,500, then $40 per 10 collections and $20 per 10,000 items | 100 GB, then $40 per 100 GB (2 TB max) | 3,000 a month |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom | Custom | Volume discounts |

A few lines from the same page change how you read that table:

- Free's 1,000 pages and 10 collections are not a typo: Framer's FAQ gives Free projects more room than Basic, and what Free withholds is the custom domain.
- Monthly billing costs more. From India the toggle moves Basic to **₹540** and Pro to **₹1,625** a month. The page's own script carries a dollar price list with $15 for Basic and $45 for Pro on monthly billing; from India the page renders rupees, so those two dollar figures come from that list rather than from the price on screen.
- Yearly billing includes extras that monthly billing does not. The comparison table says "Complimentary domain included on yearly", and the free custom domain and double credits in the first month grey out when you switch to monthly.
- Basic limits are fixed. Only Pro can buy more pages, CMS collections, CMS items or bandwidth in blocks, so a Basic site that outgrows 30 pages moves to Pro.

Framer's ladder is Free, Basic, Pro and Enterprise, and its FAQ says Basic "caters to students, freelancers, and small studios" while Pro is for teams at agencies, startups and scale-ups. ## Is Framer Free?

Yes, Framer has a Free plan at $0, and Framer's FAQ describes it as "ideal for non-commercial use". The same FAQ pitches it for exploring Framer, using it as a design tool or making templates.

What the Free plan includes, per [Framer's pricing page and FAQ](https://www.framer.com/pricing) on 25 October 2026:

| Free plan item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Domain | Free Framer domain only; a custom domain needs a paid plan |
| Bandwidth | 1 GB |
| AI credits | 500 to try, when the workspace has no active subscription |
| CMS | 10 collections |
| Pages | 1,000 |
| File uploads | 5 MB |
| Languages | One free locale to try |
| Collaboration | Up to three editors in a workspace without a subscription |
| MCP Server | Included, free during the preview |

That wording is a recommendation, not a ban; for a business site the practical limit is the domain, since connecting your own domain starts at Basic.

How the lines work, from the same page:

- The workspace owner and viewers are free. Viewers can see and comment on pages and designs.
- An additional editor can design, edit content and publish. A content editor can update the CMS, localisation and on-page text for half that price.
- Basic and Pro allow up to 10 users with edit access; Enterprise is unlimited.
- Framer's FAQ says editors are billed per seat and that you are charged within 24 hours of an editor being added.
- Translation locales cost $20 each on both Basic and Pro, up to 20 locales.
- The blog row starts from Pro's included 150 pages and 2,500 CMS items, so 200 extra pages and 10,000 extra items take it to 350 and 12,500.

Two Pro-only add-ons cost more than the plan itself: **Convert** (A/B testing, funnels and triggers) at $50 per 500,000 events, and **Advanced hosting** (multiple sites and custom headers under one domain) at $200 a month. For a year at yearly billing, multiply any monthly figure by 12: the marketing team above commits to $1,200.

## How AI Credits Change Framer's Price

Every paid plan includes monthly AI credits, which power Framer's agents, AI localisation and other AI features. Basic includes 1,000 a month and Pro 3,000, and the pricing page lets you pick a bigger credit tier, which raises the plan price: Basic runs up to 15,000 credits a month and Pro up to 100,000.

I could read the tier prices only in rupees, from an Indian connection on 25 October 2026, at yearly billing. Every tier in both selectors:

| Credits a month | Basic, ₹ per month | Pro, ₹ per month |
|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | ₹361 | - |
| 2,000 | ₹1,318 | - |
| 3,000 | ₹2,275 | ₹1,084 |
| 5,000 | ₹4,189 | ₹2,998 |
| 8,000 | ₹7,061 | - |
| 10,000 | ₹8,975 | ₹7,784 |
| 15,000 | ₹13,761 | - |
| 20,000 | - | ₹17,355 |
| 30,000 | - | ₹26,926 |
| 50,000 | - | ₹46,068 |
| 100,000 | - | ₹93,924 |

Each extra 1,000 credits added **₹957 a month** at yearly billing on both plans, which is more than 2.6 times the whole Basic plan. So for anyone who needs more than about 1,000 credits a month, Pro's included 3,000 is cheaper than Basic at 3,000 (₹1,084 against ₹2,275), before counting Pro's extra limits.

How far a credit goes, from [Framer's June 2026 announcement](https://www.framer.com/blog/ai-credits-simpler-plans-and-lower-prices/): generating a landing page costs about 300 credits on the base model, a small edit about 50, and Framer puts Basic's 1,000 credits at roughly five landing pages. The same post says AI tools you connect over MCP, such as Claude Code, Cursor or Codex, run on your own Claude or OpenAI subscription and do not draw on Framer credits. If you design on the canvas and use AI for the odd edit, the base tier is enough; buy credit tiers only for agent-heavy months.

## Framer Basic vs Pro: Which Plan Fits

Basic is for a site that will stay small; Pro is for a site with a team, a migration or a content plan. The differences from the [comparison table on framer.com](https://www.framer.com/pricing) on 25 October 2026:

| Feature | Basic | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Price, yearly billing | $10 a month | $30 a month |
| Site pages | 30, fixed | 150, buy more to 700 |
| CMS collections | 2 | 10, buy more to 40 |
| CMS items | 1,000 | 2,500, buy more to 40,000 |
| Bandwidth | 50 GB | 100 GB, buy more to 2 TB |
| Hosting locations | 20 | 300+ |
| Analytics history | 30 days | 90 days |
| Site redirects, staging, branching | No | Yes |
| Static files | No | 50 |
| Convert and Advanced hosting add-ons | No | Yes |

My decision rules:

1. Moving an existing site to Framer: take Pro, because the comparison table gives site redirects to Pro and not to Basic, and keeping old URLs alive is what protects your rankings.
2. More than 30 pages now, or within a year: take Pro, since Basic cannot buy page blocks.
3. A one-person portfolio, a link-in-bio site or a launch page: Basic is enough, and a student may get it free (see below).

## Framer Student Plan

Framer's [student page](https://www.framer.com/education/students/) offers "A free Basic plan worth $120", which matches 12 months of Basic at $10. The terms on 25 October 2026:

- Who qualifies: students enrolled in a certified in-person or online program, such as a high school, college or university.
- How long: one year, and you can re-apply every 11 months for as long as you are a student.
- What you send: name, school, school email, expected graduation date and a photo of your school ID showing your full name and the school logo.
- Review time: Framer aims to review applications within 5 working days.
- Who can redeem: for now only new customers can redeem the coupon codes, per the page's FAQ.
- Credits: the page advertises 1,000 free AI credits a month.
- Use: the terms limit the code to your personal student website, portfolio, class projects and resume.

There is no educator discount yet; the page says Framer is working on one. Figma's free education route does cover teachers: its help article gives educators two years and high school or college students one year, and [Figma's student and educator terms](/blog/figma-student-plan/) set out the reapply rules. If your coursework also needs Photoshop or Illustrator, Adobe's student route in India is a paid plan with a 7-day free trial, and [what Adobe charges students in India](/blog/adobe-student-plan-india/) covers its renewal price and the free higher-education offer a college can claim.

## What Changed in Framer's Pricing in 2026

Framer reworked its plans on 16 June 2026, alongside Framer 3.0, in the [announcement post](https://www.framer.com/blog/ai-credits-simpler-plans-and-lower-prices/) titled "AI credits, simpler plans, and lower prices":

| Change | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Paid plans | Basic, Pro and Scale | Basic and Pro (Scale retired) |
| Editor seat | $40 | $20 |
| Basic CMS collections | 1 | 2 |
| Basic bandwidth | 10 GB | 50 GB |
| AI usage | - | Credits on every plan |

The post says existing paid site plans stay as they are unless the owner switches, but the lower editor price and the new Basic limits applied to everyone right away. The Free plan's credit wording has also moved since June: the announcement described daily Free credits, while the pricing page now says "500 AI credits to try". Any guide that still lists a Scale plan or $40 editors predates June 2026.

## Price the Bill, Not the Plan

Framer pricing looks like a $10 or $30 decision, and for a solo portfolio it is. For a team, the plan is the smallest line: editors, languages and Pro's page and CMS blocks decide the total, and credit tiers can outgrow everything else. Open the page from where you will pay and check the tax at checkout before you commit. Framer changes these prices, so treat every figure here as of 25 October 2026, re-read [framer.com/pricing](https://www.framer.com/pricing) and add up plan plus seats plus locales plus blocks before you compare Framer with any other builder.
